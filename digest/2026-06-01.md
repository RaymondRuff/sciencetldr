## Top Papers

### 1. MAGE-A4/MAGE-A8-targeted TCR-based bispecific T cell engager in recurrent and/or refractory solid tumors: a phase 1 trial

Wermke M, Ochsenreither S, Jaeger D, Becker H, Bleckmann A, Bozorgmehr F, et al.

*Nature Medicine*

[10.1038/s41591-026-04455-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04455-x)

MAGE-A4/MAGE-A8 (HLA-A*02:01 peptide) × CD3 TCR-based engager (IMA401/TCER); high-affinity TCR binding domain, low-affinity T-cell-recruiting arm, half-life-extended Fc.

IMA401 is a "TCER" platform that pairs a high-affinity soluble TCR domain against an HLA-presented MAGE-A4/A8 peptide with a deliberately attenuated CD3-recruiting arm and an engineered Fc for extended half-life, decoupling target engagement strength from T-cell activation strength. In this 61-patient phase 1 interim analysis, IMA401 ± pembrolizumab was tolerable (CRS 38%, all grade 1–2) and delivered a 20% confirmed ORR at the RP2D across 15 indications, rising to 29% in head and neck cancer with an 8.8-month median DoR. Demonstrating durable single-agent responses with a TCR-based engager against an intracellular antigen in solid tumors is a meaningful validation of affinity-tuned TCER architecture where conventional CD3 bispecifics have struggled.

DICE score: 4 — A differentiated, half-life-extended TCR-based engager format with genuine solid-tumor clinical activity against an intracellular target, advancing affinity-decoupling as a design principle.

Immatics; presented at ASCO 2026 with simultaneous publication (NCT05359445).

### 2. Efficient in vivo assembly of DNA encoded multivalent BTEs for dual antigen targeting for broadening therapeutic impact in ovarian cancer

Bhojnagarwala PS, Bordoloi D, Jose JS, Tomirotti M, Ionescu C, Sharma R, et al.

*Molecular Therapy*

[10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.05.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.05.019)

FSHR × CD3 and Her2 × CD3 bispecific T-cell engagers; DNA-encoded knob-into-hole Fc heterodimers assembled in vivo from host muscle (synDNA platform).

The authors encode complementary knob and hole Fc chains on separate plasmids so that functional bispecific engagers (dKBTEs) assemble only upon co-expression in vivo, suppressing homodimer formation and enabling sustained secretion of half-life-extended BTEs directly from muscle. The platform produces FSHR × CD3 and Her2 × CD3 engagers that bind antigen and CD3, activate primary human T cells, kill ovarian tumor cells, and outperform first-generation BTEs on pharmacokinetics and tumor control in mice — with two distinct dKBTEs co-delivered for combinatorial dual-antigen coverage. This is a concrete manufacturability-and-half-life solution that sidesteps recombinant production and repeat dosing while preserving heterodimer fidelity, directly applicable to multivalent and multispecific engager delivery.

DICE score: 4 — A novel nucleic-acid-encoded in vivo assembly platform that tackles BTE half-life and manufacturing simultaneously with controlled heterodimerization, a meaningful delivery/manufacturability advance.

### 3. A first-in-class bifunctional antibody targeting CD20 and CD37 remodels the immune microenvironment in relapsed or refractory B-cell malignancies

Wang L, Liu J, Zhou K, Li Z, Zeng Y, Qian Y, et al.

*Journal of Hematology & Oncology*

[10.1186/s13045-026-01796-5](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13045-026-01796-5)

CD20 × CD37 bifunctional antibody (PSB202); dual B-cell-antigen depletion engineered to act independently of CD3 engagement.

PSB202 deliberately avoids CD3 redirection, co-targeting CD20 and CD37 to deplete malignant B cells while inducing IFN-γ release without cytokine release syndrome — a format-level attempt to decouple cytotoxic immune activation from the T-cell overactivation that constrains CD3 bispecifics. In a 15-patient phase Ia dose escalation (12–300 mg) in R/R B-NHL the MTD was not reached, the safety profile was driven by neutropenia rather than CRS, and the ORR was 30% (one CR at 300 mg) with sustained B-cell depletion. Single-cell profiling distinguishing responder and non-responder microenvironments makes this an instructive first-in-human readout for "CD3-free" dual-antigen engager design as an off-the-shelf alternative.

DICE score: 4 — A genuinely differentiated first-in-class bifunctional format that engineers out CD3-driven toxicity while retaining immune activation, with first-in-human proof of concept.

NCT05003141.

### 4. Teclistamab in Multiple Myeloma with One to Three Previous Lines of Therapy

Touzeau C, Mina R, Quach H, Hungria V, Bhutani D, Chen W, et al.

*New England Journal of Medicine*

[10.1056/NEJMoa2603870](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2603870)

BCMA × CD3 bispecific T-cell engager (teclistamab); randomized phase 3 versus standard triplets in earlier-line RRMM.

MajesTEC-9 randomized 593 RRMM patients with one to three prior lines to teclistamab monotherapy versus investigator's choice of PVd or Kd, showing a dramatic PFS benefit (18-month PFS 69.8% vs 26.9%; HR 0.29), higher ≥CR rate (65.9% vs 16.8%), and an overall survival improvement (HR 0.60). CRS was mostly grade 1–2 (66%) and grade 3–4 infections were common (41.6%), reinforcing the infection-management burden of sustained BCMA × CD3 engagement. While not an engineering paper, this randomized OS-positive readout pulls a BCMA engager firmly into earlier lines and reshapes the competitive landscape that next-generation BCMA-directed multispecifics must beat.

DICE score: 4 — Practice-shifting randomized phase 3 data moving a BCMA × CD3 engager to earlier-line therapy with an overall survival benefit.

Johnson & Johnson; MajesTEC-9 (NCT05572515).

### 5. A simple and broadly applicable nanobody-based approach to generate potent TNFR agonists

Lang I, Zaitseva O, Glöckler A, Siegmund D, Sheta D, Mouhandes B, et al.

*Cell Death & Disease*

[10.1038/s41419-026-08911-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41419-026-08911-x)

Multivalent single-domain (VHH) agonist platform; nanobody triplets genetically fused to oligomerizing scaffolds for hexa-, nona-, and dodecavalent TNFR engagement (41BB, CD40, OX40, GITR, BCMA, DR5, etc.).

The authors show that fusing single-chain nanobody triplets to oligomerizing protein scaffolds reliably converts ordinary TNFR-specific VHHs into FcγR-independent, sub-nanomolar agonists across nine TNFRSF members, decoupling agonism from the Fc-crosslinking requirement that complicates antibody agonists. The defined valency series (hexa/nona/dodecavalent) plus reported superior CMC properties give a predictable, plug-and-play route from any TNFR nanobody to a potent agonist. The oligomerization-via-scaffold logic and valency tuning are directly portable to costimulatory arms of multispecific immune engagers, where controlled higher-order valency is often the missing engineering primitive.

DICE score: 3 — A clean, broadly generalizable multivalent nanobody-scaffold format platform with portable lessons for engager costimulation and valency engineering.

Wajant group (University of Würzburg).

### 6. IAR025, a novel anti-DLL3 × CD3-bispecific antibody, displays high antitumor efficacy in preclinical models of small-cell lung cancer

Liu Y, Cao L, Xu M, Li N, Zhang J, Zhu M, et al.

*Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy*

[10.1007/s00262-026-04416-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00262-026-04416-w)

DLL3 × CD3 bispecific T-cell engager (IAR025); affinity- and epitope-optimized DLL3 arm paired with a CD3 activation arm.

IAR025 pairs an affinity- and binding-domain-optimized DLL3 arm with a CD3 arm to drive dose-dependent, antigen-specific killing of DLL3-positive SCLC cells without off-target lysis of DLL3-negative cells, and inhibits tumor growth in DMS79 and SHP-77 humanized models via enhanced intratumoral T-cell recruitment. Activity is augmented by chemotherapy or anti-PD-1, and cynomolgus tolerability up to 10 mg/kg with linear PK and a 90-h half-life supports a developable profile. In the now-crowded post-tarlatamab DLL3 × CD3 space the contribution is an incremental but well-characterized new engager with a favorable affinity-tuned profile.

DICE score: 3 — A solid, well-benchmarked new DLL3 × CD3 engager with NHP PK and combination data, though incremental within a validated target/format class.

### 7. Clinical Validation of a QSP Model for ISB 2001, a Trispecific T Cell Engager to Support Optimal FIH Study Design in RRMM Patients

Chandralayam Ayyappa Menon V, Matsuura T, Holkova B, Gudi GS, Drake A, Pihlgren M, et al.

*Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics*

[10.1002/cpt.70319](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpt.70319)

BCMA × CD38 × CD3 trispecific T-cell engager (ISB 2001); quantitative systems pharmacology model for first-in-human dose selection.

Because ISB 2001's CD3 arm does not cross-react with cynomolgus CD3, no fully cross-reactive preclinical species was available, so the authors built a QSP model to project the clinical efficacious dose range and rationalize first-in-human dosing beyond the often over-conservative MABEL approach. They then validate the model against emerging safety, PK, and antimyeloma activity from the ongoing TRIgnite-1 trial, demonstrating an efficient, model-informed escalation for a dual-TAA trispecific engager. The work is a useful methodological template for de-risking trispecific TCE dose-finding and aligns with the FDA roadmap to reduce reliance on animal testing.

DICE score: 3 — A clinically validated QSP framework for trispecific engager dose selection that meaningfully addresses the non-cross-reactive-species problem common to multispecific TCEs.

Clinical-stage; Ichnos Glenmark Innovation, TRIgnite-1 (NCT05862012).

### 8. Modified Anti-PstS1 Bi-specific antibodies unlock potent protection against tuberculosis

Akanksha, Bouzeyen R, Watson A, Wiseglass G, Sithole N, Abramovitz L, et al.

*PLoS Pathogens*

[10.1371/journal.ppat.1014133](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.ppat.1014133)

Biparatopic PstS1 bispecific antibody (Bi-S 36/163LR); two non-overlapping anti-PstS1 paratopes combined after affinity maturation of one arm.

Affinity maturation of the p4-163 arm (p4-163LR) improved PstS1 binding but, strikingly, did not by itself enhance in vivo protection against virulent M. tuberculosis — protection only emerged when the matured arm was combined with the non-overlapping p4-36 arm into a bispecific that boosted bacterial binding and antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis. The biparatopic format yielded a ~1-log lung CFU reduction where the monospecific high-affinity antibody failed, a clean demonstration that avidity/cooperativity from dual-epitope engagement can outweigh raw affinity. The mechanistic lesson — that bispecific geometry, not affinity alone, can be the determinant of function — is broadly relevant to engager and biparatopic design.

DICE score: 3 — A useful structure-guided engineering insight that bispecific dual-epitope engagement unlocks function unattainable by affinity maturation alone.

### 9. Epitope-Resolved Digital SERS Profiling of Structurally Dynamic Antigens via a Multi-Epitope Bispecific Antibody Framework

Wang J, Zhou Q, Lowry K, Howard CB, Trau M

*Advanced Science*

[10.1002/advs.75828](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.75828)

Nanobody-based bispecific fragments (EpiCount-SERS); epitope-specific VHH paired with a unified anti-mPEG conjugation domain for oriented, multiplexed display.

The platform engineers bispecific nanobody fragments that fuse an epitope-specific VHH to a shared anti-methoxy-PEG domain, enabling orientation-controlled, modular attachment to spectrally encoded SERS nanotags so that distinct epitopes on a dynamic antigen (SARS-CoV-2 RBD) are read out as independent digital channels. The "swap the binder, keep the conjugation handle" architecture solves the heterogeneous-conjugation problem that defeats simple monoclonal mixtures and achieves sub-ng/mL sensitivity and 88.3% accuracy on clinical swabs. Although the application is diagnostic, the unified-conjugation bispecific design is a reusable engineering motif for oriented, modular multispecific assembly.

DICE score: 2 — A clever modular bispecific-nanobody conjugation framework, but the contribution is a diagnostic assay rather than a therapeutic engager advance.

### 10. Cytomegalovirus reactivation associated with CD20 × CD3 bispecific T-cell engagers: a frequentist and Bayesian analysis of a large national database

Frey C

*Leukemia & Lymphoma*

[10.1080/10428194.2026.2675498](https://doi.org/10.1080/10428194.2026.2675498)

CD20 × CD3 bispecific T-cell engagers (class-level); pharmacovigilance disproportionality analysis of CMV reactivation.

This frequentist-and-Bayesian disproportionality analysis of a large national adverse-event database quantifies a CMV reactivation safety signal associated with CD20 × CD3 bispecific engagers, adding to the infectious-complication picture that increasingly shapes engager risk management. The signal is consistent with the sustained T-cell engagement and B-cell aplasia inherent to the format and argues for viral monitoring or prophylaxis strategies. There is no engineering content, but the safety signal is field-relevant for engager labeling and supportive-care design.

DICE score: 2 — A useful real-world pharmacovigilance signal for CD20 × CD3 engagers, but observational and without molecular or engineering novelty.

## Weekly Themes

This was an ASCO-2026-timed week, and the strongest signal was the arrival of affinity-decoupled and format-diversified engagers in the clinic. The headline engineering story is Immatics' IMA401, a TCR-based "TCER" that deliberately pairs a high-affinity HLA-peptide binding domain with an attenuated CD3 arm and a half-life-extended Fc, translating that affinity-tuning principle into durable single-agent responses against an intracellular MAGE-A4/A8 target in solid tumors (*Nature Medicine*) — the kind of differentiated architecture that conventional surface-antigen CD3 bispecifics have not cracked in solid disease. Two papers attacked the constraints of the classic CD3-engager format from opposite directions: a CD20 × CD37 bifunctional antibody (PSB202) that engineers CD3 out entirely to deplete B cells without CRS (*J Hematol Oncol*), and a nucleic-acid-encoded synDNA platform that assembles half-life-extended knob-into-hole BTEs in vivo to solve manufacturability and dosing burden at once (*Molecular Therapy*). Valency and avidity recurred as engineering levers — a broadly generalizable multivalent nanobody-scaffold platform turned ordinary VHHs into potent FcγR-independent TNFR agonists (*Cell Death Dis*), and a biparatopic anti-PstS1 antibody showed that dual-epitope geometry, not affinity maturation, was what unlocked protection (*PLoS Pathogens*). On the translational side, teclistamab's MajesTEC-9 randomized OS benefit pulls a BCMA × CD3 engager into earlier lines and raises the bar for next-generation BCMA multispecifics (*NEJM*), while a QSP model for the BCMA × CD38 × CD3 trispecific ISB 2001 offered a validated, animal-sparing route through the non-cross-reactive-species problem that dogs trispecific dose-finding (*Clin Pharmacol Ther*). A new DLL3 × CD3 BiTE (IAR025) and a CD20 × CD3 CMV-reactivation safety signal round out an active oncology week. Computational de novo antibody design and bona fide in-window preprints were again thin — partly a genuine lull, partly because bioRxiv remained inaccessible for date verification.
