## Top Papers

### 1. Preclinical characterization of CLSP-1025, a first-in-class, mutation-specific T-cell engager targeting a neoantigen derived from a common p53 mutation

Maloney MF, Caushi JX, Cullen LA, Andrews AR, Ford AC, Gizzi AS, et al.

*Clinical Cancer Research*

[10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1252](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-1252)

HLA-A*02:01–p53(R175H) peptide-MHC × CD3 T-cell engager (TCR-mimic binding arm).

CLSP-1025 pushes engager targeting off the cell surface and onto an intracellular driver mutation, using a TCR-mimic arm that reads the p53(R175H) neoantigen presented by HLA-A*02:01 while the CD3 arm is spaced to reconstruct a physiologic immune synapse. Killing was mutation-dependent across cell lines, patient-derived organoids, and humanized mice, and the engineering burden of this target class — off-target HLA cross-reactivity — was addressed head-on with broad HLA screens, primary tissue panels, and cytokine-release assays showing a narrow recognition profile. With IgG-like half-life and dose-proportional PK in rats and human CD3 transgenic mice, it is a credible template for extending engagers to the ~90% of the proteome that never reaches the membrane.

DICE score: 4 — A differentiated pMHC-directed engager against a high-prevalence oncogenic mutation, with the specificity package that this format has historically lacked, likely to shift how the field approaches intracellular targets.

Clasp Therapeutics; IND-enabling/clinical-stage candidate.

### 2. FcRH5×CD3 bispecific antibody cevostamab in relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma: a phase 1 trial

Cohen AD, Richter J, Trudel S, Lesokhin A, Laubach JP, Thomas SK, et al.

*Nature Medicine*

[10.1038/s41591-026-04522-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04522-3)

FcRH5 × CD3 IgG-based T-cell engager, fixed-duration dosing with triple step-up.

This 324-patient dose-escalation/expansion study is the definitive read on a non-BCMA, non-GPRC5D myeloma target: FcRH5, ubiquitously expressed on plasma cells. The MTD was not reached; at the 160 mg RP2D the objective response rate was 44.3% overall and 60.6% in BCMA-naive patients, with median duration of response of 10.4 and 19.7 months respectively — and responses persisting after the fixed ~12-month course ended. The triple step-up schedule held CRS to grade 1–2 at the RP2D, which matters as a general design lesson: dose fractionation, not molecular attenuation, was sufficient to tame cytokine toxicity for a high-density target.

DICE score: 4 — Large, mature clinical validation of a third independent myeloma antigen plus a fixed-duration, step-up dosing template with durable off-treatment remissions, directly informing target and schedule selection for next-generation engagers.

Genentech/Roche multicenter; clinical (NCT03275103).

### 3. AI-redesigned starting points and outcomes enhance protein evolution

Krasnow NA, Xu JA, Zhang E, Mahadeshwar GK, Tao YA, McCreary J, et al.

*Nature*

[10.1038/s41586-026-10820-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10820-0)

ProteinMPNN-redesigned scaffolds as starting points for directed evolution (botulinum neurotoxin proteases).

The claim here is more general than the model system: AI sequence redesign does not just stabilize a protein, it raises the local fitness landscape so that subsequent laboratory evolution reaches sequences that are simply inaccessible from the wild-type starting point. Side-by-side phage-assisted continuous evolution campaigns from redesigned versus wild-type proteases consistently favored the redesigned starts, and redesign-derived mutations failed when transplanted back into wild-type backgrounds — direct evidence that the benefit is epistatic robustness, not additive stability. For engager arms, where affinity maturation and specificity engineering are routinely run on marginal-stability scFv and VHH scaffolds, "redesign before you evolve" is an immediately actionable workflow change.

DICE score: 4 — A generalizable and rigorously controlled demonstration that AI redesign changes what directed evolution can reach, with direct implications for how binding-domain maturation campaigns are initialized.

Broad Institute / Harvard / HHMI (Liu DR lab).

### 4. Conditional activation of IL-12 through a Fibronectin-EDB dependent switch gate

Kahn JD, Parthiban K, Slavny P, Arvey A, Presler M, Nosbisch J, et al.

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2700812](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2700812)

Reversible two-arm antibody "switch gate": dual-specificity Fab switch arm (IL-12 or FN-EDB) plus an FN-EDB targeting arm.

The architecture is the contribution. A single dual-specificity Fab binds either the tethered payload or the tumor matrix antigen FN-EDB competitively, so payload release is not proteolytic and irreversible but an avidity-driven, tumor-conditional unveiling that reverses on dissociation. Binder properties were not tuned by intuition: a quantitative systems pharmacology model defined the affinity windows required for gating, and phage screening with rational library design produced switch binders hitting those specifications. This closes the loop between QSP modeling and library design for conditional biologics, and the same competitive-switch chassis is directly portable to masking a CD3 arm.

DICE score: 4 — A genuinely novel and reversible conditional-activation format, with a model-driven binder-specification workflow that generalizes well beyond the IL-12 payload used to demonstrate it.

Third Rock Ventures with IONTAS, FairJourney Biologics and Certara.

### 5. Phase 1/2 study of IMC-C103C, a T cell receptor bispecific (MAGE-A4×CD3) ImmTAC targeting MAGE-A4-expressing malignancies

Sweis RF, Melero I, Davar D, Garralda E, Hamid O, Chen HA, et al.

*Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer*

[10.1136/jitc-2025-014638](https://doi.org/10.1136/jitc-2025-014638)

MAGE-A4 peptide–HLA-A*02:01 × CD3 soluble TCR bispecific (ImmTAC).

Sixty-eight patients across ten dose-escalation cohorts and an ovarian expansion establish both the tolerability and the central limitation of the affinity-enhanced soluble-TCR format: no treatment-related discontinuations or deaths, cytokine-mediated grade 1/2 CRS as the dominant toxicity, and a lower 15-45-140 µg step-up regimen selected for expansion — but MAGE-A4 expression, while present in 71% of tumors, was low (median H-score 16). Read alongside CLSP-1025 in this same week, it sharpens the design question for pMHC-directed engagers: the format works and can be dosed safely, so the binding problem has largely become an antigen-density problem.

DICE score: 3 — Important clinical characterization of the ImmTAC platform against a cancer-testis antigen, with a clear, generalizable lesson about pMHC copy number as the limiting variable.

Immunocore-partnered multicenter; clinical (NCT03973333).

### 6. Dual bispecific protein engager-armed T cells targeting BCMA and B7-H3 overcome antigen heterogeneity in multiple myeloma

Chanpong T, Sawasdee N, Supimon K, Sangsuwannukul T, Chieochansin T, Okada S, et al.

*Biomedicine & Pharmacotherapy*

[10.1016/j.biopha.2026.119751](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopha.2026.119751)

Two bispecific protein engagers (BCMA × CD3ε and B7-H3 × CD3ε) engineered against non-overlapping CD3ε epitopes, co-armed onto the same T cells.

The engineering trick worth noting is epitope orthogonality: the two engagers are deliberately directed at distinct, non-competing CD3ε epitopes so a single T cell can be armed with both without one arm displacing the other. Functionally, dual arming broadened antigen coverage across myeloma models with heterogeneous BCMA/B7-H3 expression rather than raising intrinsic cytotoxic potency, with responses governed by antigen density, individual arm affinity, and the balance of CD3 engagement. That decomposition is the useful result — it says multispecificity buys coverage, not potency, and that CD3 epitope real estate is a finite design resource.

DICE score: 3 — A practical route to combinatorial antigen coverage via CD3-epitope-orthogonal engagers, with a clean dissection of what dual targeting does and does not contribute.

Mahidol University (Siriraj), Bangkok.

### 7. Parallel 4-1BB Signaling in Dual DLL3/CD56-Targeting CAR-T Cells Triggers Adaptive Proliferation and Functional Persistence to Eradicate Small Cell Lung Cancer

Lei YN, Qin D, He Y, Shu P, Zhang B, Chen Y, et al.

*Cancer Research*

[10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-5261](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-25-5261)

DLL3 CAR with a CD56 chimeric switch receptor and parallel 4-1BB costimulation (DBBζ.CBB), sequenced with a DLL3 trispecific T-cell engager.

Two things here matter for engager design. First, splitting 4-1BB costimulation into a parallel signaling arm rather than an in-line domain produced a staged fate program — early expansion, an intermediate highly proliferative effector state, and delayed terminal exhaustion — which is a receptor-architecture result, not just a costimulation result. Second, the authors then layer a DLL3 trispecific engager onto the intratumoral T-cell pool the CAR established, and the combination is synergistic. It is a concrete instance of the emerging "cells build the pool, engagers exploit it" combination logic in solid tumors.

DICE score: 3 — Useful architectural insight into parallel versus in-line costimulation plus a demonstration that a trispecific engager and an engineered cell product can be sequenced synergistically.

West China Hospital, Sichuan University.

### 8. A dual-tumor-recognition trispecific T-cell nano-engager enhances effector-target conjugation and antitumor activity

Ma YJ, Zhang Z, Kang JK, Che XH, Tian JY, Cai YY, et al.

*Biomaterials Science*

[10.1039/d6bm00886k](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6bm00886k)

PD-L1 × EGFR × CD3 trispecific assembled non-covalently on an FcγR1-HSA/PLLA nano-adaptor (Tri-NanoTCE).

Instead of engineering a trispecific as a single polypeptide, this work builds one by Fc-mediated capture of three intact IgG1s onto an FcγR1-displaying nanoparticle — a modular assembly route that sidesteps chain-pairing and expression problems entirely and lets the targeting composition be swapped without re-engineering the molecule. The dual tumor arms (PD-L1 and EGFR) gave complementary recognition and stronger tumor-surface anchoring, translating into better effector–target conjugation and superior killing versus free antibody mixtures or single-target nano-engagers, with prolonged intratumoral retention in PBMC-reconstituted xenografts. The trade-off — nanoparticle PK and manufacturability — is real, but the modularity is attractive for antigen-heterogeneous solid tumors.

DICE score: 3 — A genuinely alternative architecture for multispecific assembly that decouples valency and target composition from protein engineering constraints, demonstrated in vivo.

Yanbian University / Shenyang Pharmaceutical University.

### 9. Therapeutic targeting of CD47 using a tri-specific NK cell engager to inhibit multiple myeloma

Sumankan R, Sungwan P, Boonsatit N, Okada S, Panya A

*Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy*

[10.1007/s00262-026-04431-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00262-026-04431-x)

CD47 × CD16 trispecific killer engager with an embedded IL-15 moiety (TriKE-CD47).

This puts an innate checkpoint on the tumor-targeting arm rather than blocking it systemically: the CD47 arm simultaneously marks the myeloma cell and interrupts CD47–SIRPα, while the CD16 arm recruits NK cells and the IL-15 module supplies cytokine support in cis. Activity tracked CD47 expression level on target cells, and the construct enhanced both NK cytotoxicity and macrophage phagocytosis in co-culture with primary NK cells and monocyte-derived macrophages. Using a checkpoint as the targeting ligand is a neat way to convert a liability of systemic CD47 blockade — ubiquitous expression — into tumor-localized engagement.

DICE score: 3 — A well-conceived innate-engager design that fuses checkpoint blockade, NK recruitment, and cis cytokine support into one molecule, though restricted to in vitro/ex vivo demonstration.

Chiang Mai University.

### 10. A novel peptide-MHC-based T cell engager synergizes with tumor-targeted IL-12 for immunotherapy of solid tumors

Wang S, Xu Q, Peng X, Li X, Zheng T

*International Immunopharmacology*

[10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117154](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117154)

Peptide–MHC class I complex (OVA–H-2Kb) fused to an anti-HER2 nanobody, combined with a HER2-targeted IL-12 fusion.

This inverts the usual engager topology: rather than an anti-CD3 arm that recruits any T cell, the molecule presents a peptide-MHC complex that recruits only a defined antigen-specific CD8 subset to HER2-positive tumor cells. The rationale is toxicity — polyclonal CD3 agonism drives systemic activation and CRS, whereas subset-restricted recruitment engages a narrower effector pool. Pairing it with a tumor-anchored IL-12 fusion produced synergistic control in a B16-HER2 model with increased intratumoral OVA-specific CD8 responses and IFN-γ. The model antigen limits translational reach, but the "engage a chosen repertoire, not all T cells" design axis is worth tracking.

DICE score: 3 — A conceptually distinct engager topology that trades universal T-cell recruitment for repertoire selectivity, with a rational cytokine pairing, demonstrated only in a model-antigen system.

Sun Yat-sen University.

### 11. Quantum convolutional HLA immunogenic peptide prediction (Q-CHIPP): Next-generation neoantigen prediction with quantum neural network

Peters R, Rhrissorrakrai K, Bangalore Parthasarathy P, Ratner V, Gujarati TP, Tolunay M, et al.

*Science Advances*

[10.1126/sciadv.aec3824](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aec3824)

Quantum convolutional neural networks for HLA-A*02:01 9-mer binding and immunogenicity prediction.

Target selection is now the binding constraint for pMHC-directed engagers, and this work attacks it where classical models struggle: small, noisy immunogenicity datasets. A 46-qubit hardware experiment — with Pauli twirling, dynamical decoupling, and shot-controlled sampling for noise mitigation — achieved a 6% accuracy gain over classical baselines using fewer training samples, and the combined Q-CHIPP framework integrates MHC binding with T-cell recognition to better identify peptides that are actually immunogenic. Treat the quantum-advantage claim cautiously; the transferable point is that data-limited immunogenicity prediction is where a different inductive bias buys the most.

DICE score: 3 — A meaningful methodological contribution to the neoantigen/pMHC target-selection problem that gates TCR-mimic and ImmTAC engager design, tempered by narrow HLA scope and early-stage hardware.

Cleveland Clinic Center for Immunotherapy and Precision Immuno-Oncology with IBM Research.

### 12. Glycosylation penetrance of N-X-S/T sequons in antibody variable domains: a structural survey of 19,265 human antibody structures reassessing the histidine/glutamine suppression hypothesis

Gaughan CL

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2703349](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2703349)

Sequence-only developability triage for variable-domain N-glycosylation liabilities in ML-designed binders.

Machine-learning antibody design now emits candidate sequences far faster than anyone can assay their post-translational liabilities, and this survey of 1,368 variable-domain sequons across 19,265 PDB structures replaces folklore with numbers: observed penetrance is 7.82%, roughly half the estimate derived from glycosylation-enriched corpora, N-X-T sequons are 3.84-fold more susceptible than N-X-S, FR2 is glycosylation-resistant while FR1 and FR4 are elevated, and glutamine at the X position is refuted as a suppressor while histidine's suppression holds. The deliverable is a confidence-interval-aware decision tree usable as a sequence-only triage filter before anything is expressed.

DICE score: 3 — A rigorous, immediately usable developability rule set for filtering computationally designed binding domains, correcting a widely propagated overestimate of glycosylation risk.

## Weekly Themes

The dominant thread this week is **engagers pushing past the cell surface**. Two independent papers took the peptide-MHC route — a mutation-specific p53(R175H)×CD3 engager with a serious off-target HLA characterization package, and phase 1/2 data for the MAGE-A4 ImmTAC — and read together they relocate the field's bottleneck: the format is dosable and the specificity problem is tractable, but pMHC copy number is low, which is exactly why a quantum-ML framework for predicting genuinely immunogenic HLA-restricted peptides showed up in the same window. A third paper approached the same "don't engage every T cell" instinct from the opposite direction, presenting a peptide-MHC complex on the effector-recruiting arm to draw in a chosen repertoire rather than the whole CD3 compartment. **Modular and conditional architecture** was the second motif: a reversible dual-specificity-Fab switch gate whose binder affinity windows were specified by a QSP model before library design, a trispecific assembled non-covalently on an Fc-capture nanoparticle instead of as one polypeptide, and a pair of engagers deliberately aimed at non-overlapping CD3ε epitopes so both can arm the same T cell — three different ways of buying composability without paying the chain-pairing tax. Underneath ran a **binder-quality and workflow current** with unusually direct utility: Nature-level evidence that AI redesign of a scaffold changes what directed evolution can subsequently reach, and a 19,265-structure survey converting variable-domain glycosylation risk from folklore into a sequence-only triage tree — both aimed squarely at how maturation campaigns are initialized and filtered. Finally, the **clinical and safety layer** delivered a mature 324-patient readout on a third independent myeloma antigen (FcRH5) with durable off-treatment remissions under fixed-duration step-up dosing. A separate FAERS pharmacovigilance report published the same week (Am J Hematol, [10.1002/ajh.70449](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajh.70449)) is worth noting alongside it: cardiovascular events accompanied 18.4% of bispecific reports, and most hypotension and heart-failure cases arose independently of CRS — a reminder that the toxicity axis the field optimizes hardest against is not the only one.
