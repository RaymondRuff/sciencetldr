## Top Papers

### 1. NXTAGE: a phase 1/2 study of NXT007 to assess safety, pharmacokinetics, and efficacy in hemophilia A without inhibitors

Shima M, Nogami K, Matsushita T, Taki M, Sasai M, Sakaida E, et al.

*Blood*

[10.1182/blood.2025031894](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2025031894)

FIX × FX bispecific antibody (next-generation FVIIIa-mimetic; emicizumab successor) with engineered FVIII-like cofactor activity.

NXT007 is a next-generation FVIIIa-mimetic bispecific IgG with reengineered FIX and FX paratopes and isotype/charge tuning that yields markedly higher FVIII-equivalent specific activity than emicizumab, enabling Q4W subcutaneous dosing in severe hemophilia A. The phase 1/2 multiple-ascending-dose data show normal-range thrombin generation and zero treated bleeds across the higher-dose cohorts, validating the engineering hypothesis that systematically optimizing the geometry of an enzyme-mimetic bispecific can translate into a clinically differentiated, less-frequent-dosing molecule.

DICE score: 4 — A clean clinical demonstration that re-engineering a bispecific cofactor-mimetic for higher specific activity yields a tangible dosing-interval and efficacy gain over the current standard of care.

Originator: Chugai/Roche (emicizumab follow-on franchise).

### 2. Ikaros degradation by mezigdomide reduces T-cell dysfunction and improves the efficacy of antimyeloma T-cell therapies

Holthof LC, Verkleij CPM, O'Neill C, Themeli M, Mutis T, van de Donk NWCJ, et al.

*Blood*

[10.1182/blood.2025030891](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2025030891)

Mechanism-of-action study supporting CELMoD + BCMA/GPRC5D × CD3 TCE combination strategy.

Using patient bone-marrow samples, the authors map how mezigdomide-driven Ikaros/Aiolos degradation reverses the exhausted/senescent T-cell signature that limits BCMA and GPRC5D bispecific T-cell engagers in multiple myeloma, restoring cytotoxic granule machinery and proliferative capacity in TCE-redirected T cells. The dataset provides a clean molecular rationale (and patient-derived biomarker) for the wave of CELMoD-plus-TCE trials now reading out, and points at T-cell-intrinsic transcription factors as druggable nodes for engager-combination regimens.

DICE score: 4 — Mechanistic spine for the dominant emerging TCE combination strategy in myeloma, with translational immune-fitness biomarkers attached.

Originating lab: van de Donk / Mutis, Amsterdam UMC.

### 3. Tri-specific Humabody CB307 Targeting CD137, Prostate-specific Membrane Antigen, and Human Serum Albumin Whole-body Biodistribution Measured with 89Zr-CB307 PET

van Lith SAM, Heskamp S, Lobeek D, Burvenich IJG, Hoffmann RM, Legg J, et al.

*Molecular Cancer Therapeutics*

[10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-25-0610](https://doi.org/10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-25-0610)

PSMA × CD137 × HSA tri-VHH "Humabody" with first-in-human ⁸⁹Zr-PET biodistribution.

CB307 is a fully VHH-based trispecific (anti-PSMA + agonist anti-CD137 + albumin-binder for half-life) engineered as a tumor-conditional 4-1BB agonist, and this paper pairs preclinical SPECT/biodistribution with first-in-human ⁸⁹Zr-CB307 PET imaging in five PSMA-positive metastatic prostate cancer patients. The work is one of the cleanest demonstrations that an all-VHH trispecific platform can deliver tumor-selective costimulatory engagement with a quantifiable in-human PK/tissue-distribution package — directly informative for tumor-targeted CD137 agonism via multispecific engagers.

DICE score: 4 — Differentiated all-VHH trispecific architecture with rare first-in-human imaging biodistribution data validating the design intent.

Originator: Crescendo Biologics (now Avacta) / clinical-stage.

### 4. An extracellular, optogenetic antibody platform for stimulus-gated antigen recognition and modulation of cell behavior

Khamo JS, Krishnamurthy VV, Chen Y-J, Chen J, Mok B, Liu W, et al.

*Cell Chemical Biology*

[10.1016/j.chembiol.2026.04.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chembiol.2026.04.006)

"Extrabody" — split scFv/VHH antibody architecture reconstituted by light- or small-molecule-induced dimerization.

The Extrabody platform fragments antibody binders (scFv and VHH) into inactive halves that only reassemble into functional paratopes upon a defined stimulus (blue light or chemical dimerizer), demonstrated on GFP/mCherry as well as EGFR and HER2. Because reconstitution can be spatially and temporally gated, the architecture is a generalizable chassis for conditional/protected T cell engagers, masked bispecifics, and synthetic cell-cell circuits — extending the protease-masked TCE concept to non-proteolytic, externally controllable triggers.

DICE score: 4 — Generalizable conditional-activation chassis applicable to both scFv and VHH binders, opening a new axis (light/chemical) of engager spatiotemporal control.

### 5. Mutant Selective T-Cell Receptor Therapy for Solid Tumors

Chandran SS, Klebanoff CA.

*Molecular Cancer Therapeutics*

[10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-23-0894](https://doi.org/10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-23-0894)

Engineering review of mutation-specific TCRs and TCR-mimic engagers against shared neoantigens.

The authors lay out how shared driver-mutation neoantigens (KRAS G12V/D, p53, PIK3CA, β-catenin) are being attacked with affinity-matured TCRs and TCR-mimic antibodies/bispecifics, and where the field stands on HLA restriction, on-target/off-tumor risk, and TCR-mispairing solutions. The synthesis is directly relevant for engager engineers because it organizes the design choices (affinity tuning, chain pairing, HLA coverage, target validation) that determine whether a pMHC × CD3 or TCR-mimic engager can move beyond melanoma into common solid tumors.

DICE score: 3 — Field-organizing engineering review from Klebanoff (MSK) on the design space for mutant-selective TCR and TCR-mimic engagers.

Originating lab: Klebanoff lab, Memorial Sloan Kettering.

### 6. Rab5 improves CAR T cell efficacy via reducing fratricide and maintaining surface CAR levels

Gu M, Read KA, Zhao N, Posey AD, Powell DJ, June CH, et al.

*Journal of Experimental Medicine*

[10.1084/jem.20252564](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20252564)

Endocytic-trafficking architecture for CAR-T (constitutive Rab5 expression) to limit trogocytosis-driven fratricide.

The Penn group shows continuous tumor exposure drives Rab5 downregulation in CAR-Ts, breaking CAR endocytic recycling and amplifying trogocytic fratricide; constitutive Rab5 co-expression restores surface CAR levels, limits CAR capture by tumor cells, and improves antitumor activity for both BCMA-CARTs (patient-derived) and mesothelin-CARTs in solid-tumor models. Trogocytosis and CAR/binder internalization are dominant resistance mechanisms shared with T cell engagers, so Rab5-style endocytic rewiring is a portable concept for engineering "fratricide-resistant" engager-redirected T cells and CAR-engager combinations.

DICE score: 3 — Mechanistically clean, generalizable solution to trogocytosis-driven fratricide that crosses CAR-T and engager modalities.

Originating lab: June / Posey, University of Pennsylvania.

### 7. Engineered Bispecific Antibodies Achieve Broad and Potent Protection Against Multiple Ebolavirus Species

Zhang Y, Hu Y, Wang J, Li Z, Liu X, Chen Y, et al.

*Emerging Microbes & Infections*

[10.1080/22221751.2026.2668755](https://doi.org/10.1080/22221751.2026.2668755)

Cross-reactive bispecific IgG combining two non-overlapping glycoprotein paratopes for pan-ebolavirus neutralization.

A focused format-engineering study showing that pairing two distinct anti-GP arms in a single IgG-format bispecific recovers neutralization of EBOV, SUDV and BDBV that escaped from monospecific clinical-stage mAbs, and remains resilient to escape mutations that would normally disable either parent. The pattern — using bispecificity to enforce simultaneous occupancy of non-overlapping escape-distant epitopes — is directly transferable to dual-paratope tumor-targeting arms in CD3 engagers, where antigen heterogeneity and escape are the dominant resistance modes.

DICE score: 3 — Strong demonstration that bispecific dual-paratope geometry buys broader coverage and escape resistance over mAb cocktails, with read-across to oncology engagers.

### 8. Optimizing Bispecific Antibody Expression via Multi-Omics Analysis and Vector Redesign

Hennicke J, Reinhart D, Kunert R, et al.

*Biotechnology and Bioengineering*

[10.1002/bit.70228](https://doi.org/10.1002/bit.70228)

CHO manufacturability engineering for IgG-scFv bispecific format.

The authors take three near-identical IgG-scFv bispecifics with very different CHO titers, run RNA-seq plus splicing/translation analyses, and identify a 5′-UTR/ORF-context driven translational bottleneck that they fix with vector redesign — converting a low-expressing candidate into a manufacturable one. Concretely useful for protein engineers because it pinpoints the upstream RNA-level reasons IgG-scFv format bispecifics can fail CMC even when sequences look "drug-like."

DICE score: 3 — Practical, mechanistically resolved fix for a recurring IgG-scFv bispecific manufacturability failure mode.

### 9. Dual-antigen-targeting T-cell immunotherapies in MM: circumventing tumor heterogeneity and preventing antigen escape

Verkleij CPM, Frerichs KA, Wong S, O'Neill C, Themeli M, van de Donk NWCJ.

*Blood*

[10.1182/blood.2025032536](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2025032536)

Comparative review of BCMA/GPRC5D and other dual-target formats in MM (sequential vs combinatorial vs single-molecule).

Synthesizes the clinical and preclinical case for two-target T-cell redirection in myeloma — combining/sequencing BCMA and GPRC5D bispecifics, building BCMA×GPRC5D×CD3 trispecifics, and pairing TCEs with dual-target CAR-Ts — and frames the engineering trade-offs (avidity, antigen escape, target-low resistance) for each architecture. The authoritative van de Donk review is the cleanest current map of where the field's dual-engager designs converge and where they diverge.

DICE score: 3 — Authoritative, engineering-grounded synthesis of dual-target engager strategy in the most clinically advanced engager indication.

### 10. Clinical Scores to Predict Toxicities and Outcomes in Patients with Multiple Myeloma Undergoing Bispecific T-cell Engager Therapy

Frenking JH, Rade M, Hirsch P, Pott C, Goldschmidt H, Müller-Tidow C, et al.

*Blood Cancer Discovery*

[10.1158/2643-3230.BCD-25-0062](https://doi.org/10.1158/2643-3230.BCD-25-0062)

CAR-HEMATOTOX / EASIX / m-EASIX risk scores recalibrated for BCMA and GPRC5D TCEs in RRMM (n=278).

Retrospective validation showing that CAR-T-derived risk scores transfer to bispecific T-cell engager therapy in myeloma, with HTX/EASIX/m-EASIX predicting hematotoxicity, infections, response, and survival on routine pre-treatment labs. The work matters for engager design because it ties patient-intrinsic endothelial/marrow biology to TCE outcomes — providing the clinical scaffolding needed when comparing next-generation engager formats head-to-head.

DICE score: 3 — Clean clinical validation of pre-treatment risk stratification that will become standard reference for TCE benefit/risk benchmarking.

### 11. Phase I study of Y101D, a bispecific antibody targeting PD-L1 and TGF-β in patients with advanced solid tumors

Sun Y, Yang Y, Hu W, Wang Q, Hu Y, Zhang L, et al.

*The Oncologist*

[10.1093/oncolo/oyag133](https://doi.org/10.1093/oncolo/oyag133)

PD-L1 × TGF-β trap bispecific (anti-PD-L1 fused to TGFβRII ectodomain).

First-in-human dose-escalation/expansion of Y101D, a PD-L1 × TGF-β-trap bispecific, reporting MTD, PK and early response signals in chemo-refractory solid tumors. As one of several follow-on PD-(L)1×TGF-β molecules behind bintrafusp alfa, the data are most useful as a clinical benchmark for whether trap-fusion bispecifics can carve out a path in the post-bintrafusp landscape — and for calibrating expectations for similar fusion-format engagers.

DICE score: 3 — Useful first-in-human readout for the contested PD-L1×TGFβ-trap class, with PK/PD detail informing format choice.

### 12. Fc engineering of a fully humanized anti-CD147 monoclonal antibody enhances ADCC against T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia and T-lymphoblastic lymphoma

Liu Y, Chen H, Wang Z, Zhao Y, Li Y, Sun X, et al.

*Scientific Reports*

[10.1038/s41598-026-52003-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-52003-x)

Afucosylated/S239D-I332E Fc-engineered humanized anti-CD147 mAb for T-ALL/T-LBL.

The authors humanize and then Fc-engineer an anti-CD147 mAb for enhanced FcγRIIIa engagement, showing meaningful ADCC gains against T-ALL and T-LBL lines and primary blasts where therapeutic options are limited. Beyond the specific molecule, the study reinforces CD147 as a viable arm for T-cell-malignancy bispecific engagers (CD147 × CD3 / × CD16) where on-target/off-tumor risk on healthy T cells must be tightly managed.

DICE score: 3 — Practical Fc engineering vignette that nominates CD147 as a workable T-ALL target for engager-style architectures.

## Notable Preprints

### 13. A PK-Driven Quantitative Systems Pharmacology Model Predicts Cytokine Release Syndrome Severity Across T Cell-Activating Therapies via a Locked Amplification Network

Besbassi H, Vermeulen A, Snoeys J, Perez-Ruixo JJ, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.05.05.722920](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.05.722920)

Cross-class QSP model (bispecific TCE, CD28 superagonist, anti-CD3) for CRS severity.

A PK-driven mechanistic CRS model is calibrated on blinatumomab, structurally refined on TGN1412, then locked and tested blind on OKT3 — and shown to recapitulate the rank-order of CRS severity across three mechanistically distinct T cell-activating modalities via a shared macrophage-amplification network. For engager engineering it provides a transferable in silico framework to triage CD3-arm affinity and dosing schedules against CRS risk before lead selection.

DICE score: 3 — One of the first cross-modality CRS QSP models with locked-and-blind validation, directly useful for engager dose/format selection.

## Weekly Themes

The dominant signal this week was second-generation bispecific design moving from "can we hit two things?" to "can we tune the geometry, the chains, and the trigger?": NXT007 (FIX × FX, re-engineered for higher specific activity than emicizumab), CB307 (all-VHH PSMA × CD137 × HSA with first-in-human PET biodistribution), and the Extrabody split-antibody chassis all illustrate that the meaningful gains now come from format and conditional-activation engineering, not from new target pairs. A second theme is T-cell fitness as the limiting reagent for engagers: the Blood mezigdomide/Ikaros paper, the JEM Rab5/trogocytosis paper, and the Blood Cancer Discovery TCE-toxicity-score work all converge on the idea that engager efficacy is increasingly bottlenecked by T-cell-intrinsic state — and that engineering (CELMoD combos, endocytic rewiring, patient stratification) at that layer may matter more than further affinity tuning of the engager itself. Finally, the field continues to broaden its multispecific toolbox into solid tumors (mutant-selective TCR/TCR-mimic engagers, dual-paratope antibody cocktails repackaged as bispecifics) while the clinical engager pipeline keeps reading out in MM, lymphoma, and PD-(L)1 combination space.
