## Top Papers

### 1. Aberrant expression of HERV-K envelope in lymphoma represents an opportunity for novel treatments

Spriano F, Cascione L, Sgrignani J, Bendik N, Zadro A, Napoli S, et al.

*Molecular Cancer Therapeutics*

[10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-25-1542](https://doi.org/10.1158/1535-7163.MCT-25-1542)

HERV-K (HML-2) envelope protein; humanized camelid VHH reformatted as a heavy-chain-only antibody, FF-01.

The scarcest resource in engager design is not a binder but a genuinely tumour-restricted surface antigen, and this paper nominates one: an endogenous retroviral envelope protein re-expressed on the surface of a subset of diffuse large B-cell lymphomas. Locus-level HERV quantification across two independent DLBCL RNA-seq cohorts stratified patients into clinically distinct subgroups tracking an interferon/viral-mimicry programme, surface Env was confirmed by flow cytometry on patient-derived lines, and siRNA knockdown slowed proliferation, so the antigen is not a passenger. The engineering payload is FF-01, a humanized VHH that binds surface Env, drives selective ADCC in primary NK-cell assays, and internalizes rapidly on antigen engagement. That last property cuts both ways: rapid internalization is what an ADC wants, but a CD3 engager needs the antigen to persist at the synapse, so the same arm is not automatically transferable between formats.

DICE score: 4 — Supplies a tumour-restricted surface antigen with human-specific provenance plus a ready humanized single-domain arm, which is the rate-limiting input for new engager programmes rather than an incremental format tweak.

Institute of Oncology Research and IRB Bellinzona, with Robbiani, Cavalli and Bertoni.

### 2. CD3scFv-engineered AAV capsid drives superior in vitro and in vivo CAR-T cell generation and leads to complete eradication of CD19+ tumors

Chen H, Hoang T, Smith A, Shahim T, Ho CY, Selemenakis P, et al.

*Molecular Therapy*

[10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.10.035](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.10.035)

Anti-CD3 scFv grafted into an AAV6 capsid protein (VP1/VP2/VP3 variants); lead candidate AVO-100 delivering a CD19 CAR.

AAV has been written off for T cell work because natural serotypes transduce non-activated human T cells poorly, and this paper closes that gap by putting a CD3-binding arm on the capsid itself — the vector both targets and activates its own target cell. Site-selective insertion of an anti-CD3 scFv into a single capsid protein preserved production yields while raising transduction of primary human T cells more than 200-fold over unmodified AAV6; a single intravenous dose of 2×10^13 vg in NSG mice bearing Raji xenografts produced complete tumour eradication and prolonged survival. The transferable idea for engager engineers is that a CD3 paratope need not sit on a soluble protein at all: displayed on a delivery vehicle it becomes a targeting and activation module, which is the same functional role it plays in a BiTE. Caveat: the comparator is unmodified AAV6 rather than lentivirus or an optimised engineered capsid, and scFv display on capsid raises the usual immunogenicity and pre-existing-immunity questions that the abstract does not address.

DICE score: 4 — Converts the CD3-binding arm from a soluble engager component into a capsid-displayed targeting module, with a 200-fold transduction gain and complete in vivo tumour clearance from one dose.

Virovek.

### 3. Trophoblast cell-surface antigen 2-directed enhancement of gene delivery using a nanobody-engineered recombinant adeno-associated virus for bispecific T-cell engager-mediated T-cell cytotoxicity in triple-negative breast cancer cells

Shin HG, Yang HR, Hwang JH, Cho HR, Hwang K, Lee S

*International Journal of Biological Macromolecules*

[10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2026.155185](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2026.155185)

TROP2-targeting nanobody K118.1 inserted into the GH2/GH3 loop of AAV6 VP1; vector payload is a BiTE transgene.

The mirror image of the previous paper: instead of aiming the capsid at T cells, aim it at the tumour and let the tumour secrete the engager. Phage display yielded K118.1, a nanomolar-affinity TROP2 nanobody that internalizes on binding and cross-reacts with human and mouse TROP2 — the cross-reactivity is the quietly important part, since it allows syngeneic and xenograft work with one reagent. Grafting it into the AAV6 VP1 GH2/GH3 loop raised BiTE transgene expression 3- to 6-fold in TROP2-positive cells over parental rAAV6, with GFP reporter work confirming that TROP2 engagement rather than generic avidity drives the gain, and the delivered BiTE produced robust T cell activation and killing in vitro. Epitope mapping and structural modelling place K118.1 on a conformation-dependent hTROP2 epitope. The in vitro-only endpoint is the clear limit: 3- to 6-fold is a modest window for a capsid-retargeting strategy, and nothing here tests whether local BiTE secretion stays local.

DICE score: 4 — Pairs a newly isolated internalizing TROP2 nanobody with capsid loop engineering to make tumour cells the manufacturing site for an engager, a composition-and-delivery route rather than another binding improvement.

### 4. Rational design of dosage compensated expression vectors for multi-subunit biologics manufacturing

Kallappagoudar S, Yilmaz S, Niu X, Zhang Q, Sobreira F, Tang X, et al.

*Biotechnology Progress*

[10.1002/btpr.88566](https://doi.org/10.1002/btpr.88566)

Fab + scFv bispecifics and knob-into-hole IgG-like multi-subunit molecules expressed in CHO.

Chain imbalance is the unglamorous reason bispecific programmes stall, and this is a systematic treatment of it rather than another case study. Mining discovery-stage CHO co-transfection data from monocistronic vectors, the authors show subunit expression balance correlates strongly with product purity across both mAbs and bispecifics, and that Fab/scFv bispecifics have a characteristic pathology: the scFv chain is routinely overexpressed relative to heavy chain, dragging purity down. Raising heavy-chain expression — in the simplest case an extra HC gene copy — improved purity and titre across multiple programmes, and for a more complex IgG-like molecule simply reordering the knob and hole heavy-chain genes within bicistronic vectors rebalanced expression and lifted purity. The proposal is to use early monocistronic profiling as the design input for multicistronic vector construction, which turns a cell-line-development trial-and-error step into something predictable.

DICE score: 4 — Diagnoses a recurring, format-specific chain-imbalance failure mode in bispecific expression and gives a cheap design rule for fixing it before cell-line development rather than after.

Merck & Co.

### 5. Non-ensemble, conformation-resolved stability analysis of trispecific antibodies by differential melting voltage

Liu FC, Mayberry KA, Lee J, Bleiholder C, Gunawardena HP

*Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry*

[10.1021/jasms.6c00316](https://doi.org/10.1021/jasms.6c00316)

Trispecific antibody, wild-type versus an Fc-mutated ("AAS") variant; antigens undisclosed.

The uncomfortable finding here is that the standard developability readouts cannot tell two trispecifics apart when they plainly differ. Under native conditions both constructs gave compact structures with nearly identical ensemble-average collision cross sections, and solution-phase nanoDSF reflected the difference only weakly. Tandem-trapped ion mobility with differential melting voltage analysis resolved discrete conformational subpopulations and assigned each a single stability metric: the wild-type construct showed a stepwise increase in melting voltage across subpopulations of comparable stability, while the Fc-mutated variant showed stability rising gradually with collision cross section — two distinct conformational landscapes behind one bulk thermogram. For multispecifics, where chain pairing and domain orientation generate exactly this kind of heterogeneity, the implication is that equal intact mass and equal bulk thermal behaviour do not license the conclusion that two candidates are equivalent.

DICE score: 3 — Shows ensemble thermal metrics hide the conformational subpopulations that matter in trispecifics, and supplies a single-number per-subpopulation alternative.

Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine.

### 6. Complementary ligand binding assay and intact mass spectrometry strategies for preclinical pharmacokinetics of trispecific antibody candidates

Melby JA, Karmele EP, Levesque I, Wang J, Chen Z, Johansson R, et al.

*Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry*

[10.1021/jasms.6c00312](https://doi.org/10.1021/jasms.6c00312)

Monospecific IgG1 control, "Duet VHH4" (a VHH-containing trispecific DuetMab), and "Trimab" (a trispecific mAb).

Mouse PK with two orthogonal readouts, and the format comparison alone is worth the paper: exposure ranked Trimab > IgG1 > Duet VHH4, meaning the VHH-containing trispecific was the worst-exposed molecule in the set. A ligand-binding assay quantified total concentration for all three; intact LC-MS confirmed the predominant circulating species stayed structurally intact across the study and additionally resolved chain mispairing and glycation in vivo — features invisible to LBA and destroyed by the peptide-digestion workflows usually used for bioanalysis. The argument is that for multispecifics, concentration and integrity are separate questions, and only the second tells you whether the molecule circulating at hour seventy-two is the one you designed. Caveat: single-species, undisclosed targets, and no attempt to attribute the Duet VHH4 exposure deficit to a specific structural cause.

DICE score: 3 — Makes in vivo structural fidelity a measurable, format-discriminating attribute for trispecifics instead of an assumption carried over from mAb bioanalysis.

AstraZeneca.

### 7. Tarlatamab for treatment of small cell lung cancer in clinical practice: a multicenter U.S. retrospective cohort study from the ONWARD consortium

Joshi U, Zhang B, Thompson ZJ, Desai A, Naqash AR, Green AL, et al.

*Journal of Thoracic Oncology*

[10.1016/j.jtho.2026.106243](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtho.2026.106243)

Tarlatamab, DLL3 × CD3 half-life-extended bispecific T cell engager.

The largest real-world dataset yet for a half-life-extended engager, and it corrects the trial picture in both directions. Across 265 patients at 11 US centres, with 44.5% carrying new or progressive brain metastases at baseline, disease control was 52.6% in the evaluable cohort, median real-world PFS 2.3 months and OS 8.0 months, with PFS indistinguishable by baseline brain metastasis (2.2 versus 2.3 months). Toxicity ran above trial rates — CRS in 40.8% (3.8% grade 3-4) and ICANS in 21.5% (6% grade 3-4), both falling sharply after the first doses — and the striking signal is prophylactic tocilizumab: 5.3% versus 43.0% CRS, no ICANS at all versus 22.4%, and less prolonged hospitalization. With only 19 prophylaxis patients against 237 this is association, not evidence of causation, but it is the kind of association that should be tested prospectively rather than argued about.

DICE score: 3 — Real-world toxicity and efficacy at scale for a half-life-extended engager, plus a prophylactic-tocilizumab signal large enough to change how step-up dosing is managed.

Clinical-stage: approved agent, multicentre US consortium led from Moffitt.

### 8. TAVO412, an anti-EGFR, cMET and VEGF trispecific antibody: initial results from a phase I dose escalation study in patients with advanced solid tumors

Han C, Xie H, Yu Y, Zhang W, Chiu ML

*The Oncologist*

[10.1093/oncolo/oyag444](https://doi.org/10.1093/oncolo/oyag444)

EGFR × cMET × VEGF-A trispecific antibody.

First-in-human data for a three-target antibody is still rare enough that the tolerability result is the news. Across 67 patients and six tumour types, dose escalation from 150 to 1500 mg every two weeks produced no dose-limiting toxicities and no MTD, with dose-proportional exposure and immunogenicity of only 5.9% — a reassuring number for a molecule carrying three binding specificities, since anti-drug antibodies are the usual tax on added paratopes. Adverse events were largely target-related (rash, hypoalbuminemia, stomatitis, infusion reactions). Activity in heavily pretreated patients was modest but real: five partial responses among 27 NSCLC patients, one of 11 in ESCC, four of 15 in CRC, mostly at 750-1500 mg, with baseline soluble VEGF-A correlating with response. This is not a T cell engager, but it is direct evidence about whether trispecific architecture survives contact with patients.

DICE score: 3 — Clinical tolerability and low immunogenicity for a three-specificity antibody up to 1500 mg, which is the practical question hanging over every trispecific engager design.

Clinical-stage: NCT06761651.

### 9. Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay-based screening approach for phage-independent affinity maturation of single-domain antibodies

Wada S, Ito T, Nakazawa H, Umetsu M

*Journal of Bioscience and Bioengineering*

[10.1016/j.jbiosc.2026.10.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiosc.2026.10.003)

Anti-procalcitonin VHH; CDR3-focused library screened by ELISA without display.

Phage display imposes a selection bias nobody wants and a re-cloning step nobody enjoys, and this paper removes both. Single-residue random mutation scanning first located functional hotspots within CDR3; a library combining mutations only at those positions was then screened directly by ELISA, yielding a variant with 70-fold improved KD over wild type. The authors then fed the screening data into a Bayesian-optimization model to find further high-affinity variants — the point being that an assay-native workflow produces quantitative, model-ready data, whereas display enrichment discards most of what it measures. For multispecific work, where VHH arms are the usual compact building block and where display-derived affinity can shift once the domain is reformatted, a maturation loop run in the assay format you actually care about is the more trustworthy route. The target is a biomarker rather than a tumour antigen, so generality across harder epitopes is asserted, not shown.

DICE score: 3 — A display-free, ELISA-native VHH maturation loop closed with Bayesian optimization, giving 70-fold affinity gain and reusable data rather than discarded enrichment rounds.

### 10. Clonal tree frequency analysis reveals affinity determinants and enables antibody lead optimization

Waterhouse M, Gu S, Tsoumpeli M, Dugan B, Sandhu K, Ciminieri C, et al.

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2755659](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2755659)

Anti-ST2 monoclonal antibody clonal family; IL-33/ST2 axis.

Within one clonal family V(D)J recombination is fixed and only somatic hypermutation varies, which makes the family a natural experiment on which mutations govern affinity — this work reads it as one. Amino acid frequency analysis across a large phylogenetic clonal tree of anti-ST2 antibodies identified M33I and L100I in the heavy chain as the affinity-governing substitutions, corroborated by AlphaFold2 modelling and molecular dynamics free-energy decomposition. The I33/I100 genotype predicted better functional neutralization of IL-33-induced IL-6 release and tracked a higher transcriptomic affinity score. Prospective mutagenesis of three low-affinity parent antibodies then delivered up to 10-fold affinity improvement, which is the part that makes this prescriptive rather than retrospective. The constraint is that the method needs a deeply sampled clonal family, so it suits campaigns with repertoire data in hand rather than a single lead from a synthetic library.

DICE score: 3 — Turns repertoire phylogeny into a prospective affinity-maturation tool with 10-fold confirmed gains, usable on engager binding arms where clonal families have been sequenced.

AstraZeneca Biologics Engineering.

### 11. Blinatumomab in dermatomyositis and refractory ANCA-associated vasculitis: a 2-patient exploratory study

Lu C, Jin X, Dai J, Shen N, Ma S, Xu X, et al.

*Annals of the Rheumatic Diseases*

[10.1016/j.ard.2026.10.3160](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ard.2026.10.3160)

Blinatumomab, CD19 × CD3 bispecific T cell engager, given as low-dose continuous infusion for two cycles.

Engagers are migrating out of oncology, and the dosing question that migration raises is whether you need the oncology exposure at all. Two patients — one anti-Mi-2-positive dermatomyositis, one refractory ANCA-associated vasculitis — received low-dose blinatumomab over two cycles, with flow cytometry and single-cell RNA sequencing tracking immune dynamics across 14 months. Rapid T cell activation and peripheral B cell depletion came with clinical improvement in both and, notably, without cytokine release syndrome or other serious adverse events. By week 12 both T and B compartments had returned toward homeostasis, with reduced B cell activation and downregulated proinflammatory genes in myeloid cells, and clinical benefit persisted through follow-up. With n=2 this establishes nothing about efficacy, but the immunological trajectory — deep depletion followed by reset rather than prolonged aplasia — is the observation worth designing the next study around.

DICE score: 3 — Longitudinal single-cell immunology for a low-dose engager regimen in autoimmunity, suggesting the therapeutic window there may sit well below oncology exposure.

Clinical-stage: approved agent used off-label; Zhejiang University, Sir Run Run Shaw Hospital.

### 12. Direct infusion intact mass spectrometry for rapid and sensitive characterization of antibody therapeutics in complex matrices

Kristensen DB, Beck A, Coll-Satue C, Nicolas A, Bendz M, Eskesen NS, et al.

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2761105](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2761105)

Format-agnostic; demonstrated on ADC serum stability and on early-stage bispecific antibody clone development.

Intact mass spectrometry normally carries a chromatography column and the throughput penalty that comes with it. This workflow drops the column entirely, pairing off-line 96-well affinity purification with direct infusion to reach roughly one minute per sample on under a microlitre, with detection in the low µg/mL range and linearity of R≥0.99 across 1.953-500 µg/mL. Two demonstrations carry it: tracking drug-load distribution and average drug-to-antibody ratio for an ADC in mouse serum, and screening bispecific clones early in development, where the deconvoluted signal ranked clone quality in a way that anticipated performance in expanded fed-batch culture. For bispecific programmes the attraction is ranking hundreds of clones on molecular quality rather than titre alone, using commercial instrumentation and software. The method reports relative quality, not absolute structural assignment, and column-free operation gives up the separation that resolves co-eluting species.

DICE score: 3 — Makes intact-mass quality ranking fast and cheap enough to use as a primary bispecific clone-selection filter rather than a late characterization step.

Servier Symphogen, with Pierre Fabre.

## Notable Preprints

### 1. A biomimetic SPARC platform coupling adaptor recruitment and BRS-Lck condensation for enhanced NK/T cell combination therapy

Wang Q, Wu S, Qiu Y, Lou J, Lou C, Chen H

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.02.763574](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.02.763574)

Lymphoma surface antigen × engineered extracellular domains grafted onto NK cell receptor 1 and the TCR ζ chain; signalling through endogenous CD3ζ/FcRγ adaptors.

The design premise is that a receptor should recruit the cell's own signalling adaptors rather than carry a fused ITAM tail, which is what gives TCR-fusion formats their lower tonic signalling. What makes this more than another synthetic receptor is the mechanism: the NCR1 cytoplasmic domain forms condensates with Lck through two conserved basic-rich sequences, and that phase behaviour — not additional ITAMs — drives receptor clustering and raises cytotoxicity. The platform is portable to any immune cell expressing CD3ζ or FcRγ, and SPARC-engineered T cells combined with SPARC-engineered NK cells outperformed either alone. For engager work the interesting handle is upstream of binding entirely: condensation as a tunable amplifier between cluster formation and signal output.

DICE score: 4 — Identifies phase separation at a defined sequence motif as a designable gain stage in immune receptor signalling, which is the step an engager-induced cluster has to pass through.

Institute of Biophysics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

### 2. Transferrin receptor binding valency determines the circulating half-life and weight-loss efficacy of Fc-FGF21 in TfR-humanized mice

Zhou H, Deng Y, Li H, Meng J, Straub LG, Zhao S, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.02.763770](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.02.763770)

Human transferrin receptor × FGF21; bivalent Fc homodimer versus a bispecific Fc with a single hTfR arm.

A clean negative result on valency, done in the only system where it can be read properly. In wild-type mice the engineered hTfR arm is inert and the molecule behaves like plain Fc-FGF21; in homozygous hTfR-humanized mice the bivalent construct failed to induce any weight loss at all, because target engagement collapsed its circulating half-life. Rebuilding it as a monovalent bispecific Fc with one hTfR arm partially restored both half-life and efficacy, while dropping to heterozygous receptor expression did not — so this is avidity-driven clearance, not a simple receptor-dose effect. Monovalent-arm engineering to blunt target-mediated disposition is a known trick; the contribution is a controlled demonstration that bivalency alone can destroy a multispecific's exposure even when the second arm is only a shuttle, which is the decision every 2+1 engager architecture makes early.

DICE score: 4 — Quantifies how a second copy of a non-payload binding arm can abolish efficacy through target-mediated clearance, with the monovalent bispecific rescue in the same humanized model.

Baylor College of Medicine.

### 3. Decoding antibody-antigen recognition: the BL8 language and its application in calibrating deep learning predictions

Miao T, Zhu R, Lin R, Qian J, Feng Y, Chen S, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.30.763264](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.30.763264)

PD-L1, PD-1 and SARS-CoV-2 RBD as test beds; an eight-view interpretable structural description of antibody-antigen interfaces.

Cofolding models now return confident predictions faster than anyone can test them, and confidence is not viability. This work builds a fixed reference atlas of 1,498 discrete interface signatures from 15,396 experimental records in SAbDab, then projects 28,501 complexes predicted by AlphaFold 3, Protenix-v2 and OpenDDE into that space. Used as a calibration layer the atlas decoupled physical plausibility from model confidence and rejected 15.3-20.1% of highly deceptive false positives, exposing cross-target hallucinations that ipTM-style scores pass. It also let the authors extract multi-model consensus on out-of-distribution binding modes. The descriptors are interpretable and the filter is orthogonal to confidence, which is what makes it usable as a gate rather than another score to average.

DICE score: 3 — An interpretable, confidence-orthogonal rejection filter for cofolded antibody-antigen complexes with a quantified false-positive catch rate across three current models.

### 4. FoldAbS: repurposing the protein folding model as a foundation encoder for antibody screening

Wu J, Wu F, Zhu X, Huang D, Cheng K, Ma J, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.03.763899](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.03.763899)

Method; a frozen folding model (Protenix) used as an encoder with lightweight heads for specificity, affinity and developability.

Most in-silico antibody triage runs three disconnected models and throws away the structural information that governs recognition in the first place. FoldAbS instead freezes a folding model and extracts its internal representations — sequence, geometry, interaction dynamics — rather than only its output structures or confidence scores, then trains small task heads on three therapeutic objectives at once. The Protenix instantiation improved on baselines by up to 13.1% on specificity, 9.2% on affinity and 6.2% on developability, with code released. Treating a cofolder as a frozen feature extractor is becoming a recognised pattern; doing all three axes in one architecture and reporting developability — the axis most pipelines quietly skip — is the part worth borrowing for engager arm triage.

DICE score: 3 — Co-optimizes the three properties an engager arm actually has to satisfy from one frozen folding-model encoder, with released code and benchmark-relative gains on each.

Tencent.

### 5. De novo design of mini-proteins as specific binders to PACAP-38, a key mediator of migraine

Yamaguchi H, Suzuki S, Fujiyoshi Y, Isalan M

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.30.763049](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.30.763049)

PACAP-38 (a small flexible vasoactive peptide); 63-residue de novo miniproteins from BindCraft, redesigned with ProteinMPNN.

Designing against a short flexible peptide rather than a rigid globular epitope is the hard case, and the regime closest to peptide-MHC and to disordered tumour antigens. BindCraft produced ICLIST-2 at KD 26.5 nM; alanine scanning plus structural analysis nominated positions for redesign, and ProteinMPNN returned ICLIST-11 with nine substitutions at KD 4.51 nM — roughly 6-fold better — alongside a 14 °C higher Tm, with molecular dynamics attributing both gains largely to the same three hydrophobic substitutions (E30F, M31L, R54W). Functionally ICLIST-11 neutralized PACAP-38-induced PAC1R activation at IC50 10.6 nM. Affinity and stability co-improving from the same mutations is the result, and it sits in useful tension with this week's anti-TIGIT affibody work where language-model-guided stabilization bought 4.4 °C at the cost of 2-fold affinity.

DICE score: 3 — A complete, wet-lab-validated design loop against a flexible peptide target, with affinity and thermostability improving together and the responsible residues identified.

Imperial College London.

## Weekly Themes

Nobody posted a new engager architecture this week — no new BiTE, tandem scFv, trispecific engager or NK engager appeared on bioRxiv or medRxiv in the window, and the peer-reviewed column produced no new CD3-engager format with affinity and potency characterization either. What filled the gap is revealing: the week's engager-relevant work clustered almost entirely on the parts of a programme that come before and after binding. Four of the top five papers are about expression, conformation, exposure and quality attributes of multispecifics rather than about what they bind. The dosage-compensation work names a specific recurring pathology — scFv chains outrunning heavy chains in Fab/scFv bispecifics — and fixes it with gene copy number and gene order; the trispecific Tandem-TIMS study shows that two constructs with identical bulk thermograms can have entirely different conformational landscapes; the trispecific PK study finds a VHH-containing format with the worst exposure in its set and detects chain mispairing in circulation. Read together they say the same thing from three directions: for a multi-paratope molecule, the ensemble measurements inherited from monoclonal antibody development are not sufficient to tell two candidates apart.

The valency question got its sharpest answer in a paper that has nothing to do with engagers. In TfR-humanized mice, a bivalent shuttle arm did not merely reduce exposure, it abolished efficacy outright, and converting to a single arm on a bispecific Fc partially restored both — with receptor zygosity ruling out a simple dose effect. That is the cleanest available demonstration that an extra copy of a non-payload binding arm is a pharmacokinetic liability rather than a free avidity gain, and it belongs in the file of anyone choosing between 1+1 and 2+1 geometry. The same theme of hidden cost recurs elsewhere: the HERV-K VHH internalizes rapidly, which is a virtue for an ADC arm and a problem for a CD3 engager that needs its antigen to stay at the synapse, and the anti-TIGIT affibody campaign reported stabilization bought at the price of affinity, where this week's PACAP-38 miniproteins got both from the same three mutations. Property trade-offs remain local and construct-specific, not general.

Delivery was the week's most active front, and in both directions. One group grafted an anti-CD3 scFv onto an AAV capsid to make the vector target and activate T cells for in vivo CAR generation; another grafted a TROP2 nanobody into a capsid loop so tumour cells become the factory for a secreted BiTE. In both the binding domain has stopped being a component of a soluble drug and become a targeting module on a vehicle — a continuation of the in vivo engager-expression trend visible in recent weeks, now with single-domain and scFv arms doing capsid-retargeting duty. Against that, the SPARC preprint goes the other way and asks what happens downstream of clustering, locating signal amplification in Lck condensation at two basic-rich motifs rather than in ITAM count. Between capsid-displayed paratopes and condensate-tuned signalling, the binding event itself is looking less like the interesting part of the problem.

On the computational side the week was about distrust. The BL8 interface atlas exists because cofolding confidence scores pass complexes that are physically implausible, and it rejects 15-20% of the most deceptive ones; FoldAbS repurposes a folding model as a frozen encoder precisely to stop running specificity, affinity and developability as three disconnected predictions; CARTnet's absolute AUPRC of around 0.25 on de novo B-cell epitope prediction is an honest reminder of how unsolved that problem remains, even as one template residue lifts it to 0.40. The wet-lab side of design looked healthier than the benchmark side: BindCraft-then-ProteinMPNN against a flexible peptide, and a display-free ELISA-native VHH maturation loop that fed its screening data into Bayesian optimization instead of discarding it. Clinically, the ONWARD tarlatamab cohort delivered the most consequential number of the week — CRS in 5.3% with prophylactic tocilizumab versus 43.0% without, in only 19 patients, but a signal that deserves a prospective test — while blinatumomab at low dose in two autoimmune patients produced depletion followed by immune reset with no CRS at all, hinting that the therapeutic window outside oncology may sit considerably lower than the one engagers were built for.
