## Top Papers

### 1. Logic-gated T cell engager with mutant IL-2 potentiates antitumor responses without cytotoxicity in solid tumor models

Li S, Li N, Liu X, Cai Y, Wang M, Wang H, et al.

*The Journal of Clinical Investigation*

[10.1172/JCI204657](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI204657)

Tumour antigen × CD3 in a protease-activatable dual-variable-domain pro-antibody, fused to a triple-mutant IL-2 (DVD-3E).

The usual reading of poor engager performance in solid tumours is insufficient T cell numbers or antigen access; this group argues instead that the limiting signal is cytokine support, since a redirected T cell gets TCR signalling with no IL-2 to sustain it. Two separate attenuations are stacked into one molecule: the anti-CD3 arm is buried in a dual-variable-domain format that only opens on tumour protease cleavage, and the IL-2 moiety carries three mutations that cut receptor affinity enough to make it inert in circulation, so it only works where it is concentrated in cis alongside an engaged CD3. The consequence worth noting is mechanistic rather than merely tolerability-related: cis IL-2 plus TCR cosignalling expanded TCF1-positive tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes rather than just driving the existing effectors harder, which is a different durability argument than potency tuning. It remains a mouse study, and protease-dependent unmasking carries the familiar risk that normal tissue proteases blunt the gate.

DICE score: 5 — Combines conditional CD3 unmasking with affinity-detuned cis-acting IL-2 in a single molecule, defining a reusable design principle for cytokine-supported engagers rather than a single better construct.

Changping Laboratory and Tsinghua University, Yang-Xin Fu group.

### 2. Etentamig in relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma: pooled phase 1 data of an investigational BCMA bispecific antibody

Baljevic M, Voorhees P, Rodriguez C, D'Souza A, Chung A, Lichtman EI, et al.

*Blood*

[10.1182/blood.2026034590](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2026034590)

BCMA × CD3 IgG-like bispecific with a deliberately low-affinity CD3 arm, a bivalent high-avidity BCMA arm, and retained FcRn binding.

This is the clearest clinical test so far of the asymmetric-affinity thesis for BCMA engagers: put the avidity on the tumour arm, detune the CD3 arm, and keep Fc recycling so the molecule can be dosed monthly. Across one hundred and forty-six patients with at least three prior lines, objective responses occurred in sixty-six per cent and very good partial responses or better in fifty-four per cent, with median duration of response and progression-free survival not reached at thirteen months of follow-up. The number that speaks to the design is the cytokine release syndrome rate in the phase 1b arm — thirty per cent, all grade one or two, with a single step-up dose and modified dexamethasone premedication, plus grade three or four infection in twenty-three per cent of the pooled population, which is lower than the field has come to expect from BCMA engagers. Pooled early-phase data with changing schedules cannot settle whether this is the CD3 detuning or the premedication, but it is the right experiment to have run.

DICE score: 4 — Clinical evidence that detuning the CD3 arm while keeping tumour-arm avidity and FcRn recycling buys a monthly schedule and a lower CRS burden without giving up depth of response.

Clinical-stage (NCT03933735, NCT05650632); AbbVie.

### 3. VEGF-4-1BB conditional bifunctional antibody drives tumor-localized immunity through sustained macrophage and T-cell activation

Zhou X, Yen M, Brady JR, Phat SR, Yvon O, Bray JA, et al.

*Cancer Immunology Research*

[10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1380](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-25-1380)

VEGF × 4-1BB bifunctional antibody (AMP-410) in which 4-1BB agonism is conditional on VEGF binding.

Systemic 4-1BB agonism has a well-documented hepatotoxicity ceiling, and the usual fix is a tumour-antigen anchor. Here the anchor is VEGF itself, which is both neutralised and used as the localisation cue, so costimulation switches on where VEGF concentration is high — a soluble-ligand-gated conditional agonist rather than a cell-surface-gated one. The immunology is the interesting part for engager designers: the molecule drove early M1 macrophage differentiation alongside expansion of progenitor-exhausted T cells, with clonal T cell expansion confined to the tumour and little exchange with draining lymph nodes, and it beat urelumab on long-term protection and liver tolerability and a VEGF-PD-1 bispecific without the systemic interferon-gamma spike. Gating on a secreted factor is a real departure from the tumour-antigen paradigm, though it inherits that factor's distribution, and the measured intratumoural VEGF concentrations are the load-bearing translational data.

DICE score: 4 — Uses a soluble angiogenic factor rather than a surface antigen as the conditional switch for costimulation, which opens a gating strategy available to engagers lacking a clean tumour antigen.

Amplitude/industry group with human 4-1BB transgenic model work.

### 4. Natural ligand-based CAR T-cell therapy targeting BCMA and TACI for relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (TriPRIL): a non-randomised, phase 1, first-in-human trial

Puliafito BR, Graham CE, Raje NS, Yee AJ, Cirstea DD, Branagan AR, et al.

*The Lancet*

[10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01806-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01806-4)

BCMA and TACI dual recognition through an engineered trimeric APRIL binding domain, in an autologous CAR rather than a soluble engager.

The binding domain, not the cell product, is what makes this relevant to engager engineering: instead of pairing two scFvs, the targeting moiety is a trimerised version of APRIL, the natural ligand shared by BCMA and TACI, so one module covers two antigens whose joint loss is far less likely than loss of BCMA alone. The trimeric format matters because monomeric APRIL constructs have historically underperformed, and this first-in-human dose-escalation and expansion study in heavily pretreated patients — including prior CAR T-cell and bispecific exposure — establishes safety, tolerability and a recommended phase 2 dose. For anyone designing against antigen escape, the transferable lesson is that a natural multivalent ligand can replace a two-paratope architecture and sidestep the usual pairing and developability problems, at the cost of inheriting whatever affinity nature settled on.

DICE score: 4 — Clinical validation that an engineered trimeric natural ligand can serve as a dual-antigen binding module, a route to escape-resistant targeting that transfers directly to soluble engager formats.

Clinical-stage, first-in-human; Massachusetts General Hospital.

### 5. A rapidly internalizing, tetravalent antibody-drug conjugate leads to high selectivity in T-ALL cell lines due to surface antigen depletion

Abdellatif M, Rauschmayer M, Willier S, Schwach J, Kydd SM, Mehra D, et al.

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2764025](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2764025)

CD7-targeted tetravalent, low-monovalent-affinity antibody-drug conjugate; the mechanism, not the payload, is the contribution.

Avidity-based selectivity is the standard answer to on-target off-tumour toxicity, and this paper shows cleanly why it is not enough: cells with low overall antigen but dense local patches still get avidly bound, so the therapeutic window closes. The alternative proposed here is kinetic rather than thermodynamic. A tetravalent, fast-internalising construct binds CD7-high and CD7-low cells with comparable strength but strips the surface antigen pool so quickly that total uptake is capped by how much antigen a cell had to begin with — low expressers run out of surface target before they can internalise a lethal dose. That reframes valency and internalisation rate as a joint selectivity lever, which is directly relevant to engagers built on the same low-affinity multivalent logic, although an engager needs the antigen to persist long enough to hold a synapse, so the mechanism may cut the other way there.

DICE score: 4 — Identifies antigen-depletion kinetics as a selectivity mechanism distinct from avidity, and shows the two can be traded against each other by format design.

LMU Munich with Subklewe and Feuchtinger groups.

### 6. Engineered outer membrane vesicle nanoengagers potentiate NK cell immunotherapy through immune bridging and mitochondrial apoptosis priming

Yin S, Yu J, Li J, Wang Y, Li R, Tian F, et al.

*Molecular Therapy*

[10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.11.033](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2026.11.033)

Bispecific NK engager (TKD-Huhf) displayed on *E. coli* outer membrane vesicles through a matrix-metalloproteinase-2-cleavable membrane anchor.

Two things are being combined that are usually pursued separately: the engager is immobilised on a bacterial vesicle that is itself an adjuvant and a tumour-sensitising agent, and it is released only where matrix metalloproteinase 2 activity is high. The vesicle lowers the apoptotic threshold of the target cell — cytochrome c release, MCL-1 downregulation, mitochondrial depolarisation — while the liberated engager bridges NK cell to tumour cell, so the construct pairs death sensitisation with synapse formation in the same place and at roughly the same time. Tumour control in A20 lymphoma and 4T1 breast models came with increased NK and T cell infiltration. Bacterial vesicles bring their own immunogenicity and manufacturability questions, and the protease-gated anchor has the same specificity caveat as every other protease switch, but using the carrier as a pharmacologically active component rather than inert scaffolding is a genuinely different framing.

DICE score: 3 — Makes the delivery vehicle do pharmacological work alongside the engager, with protease-gated release coordinating target sensitisation and immune bridging.

Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

### 7. Allogeneic trifunctional NK cell therapy combined with rituximab for refractory mixed class IV/V lupus nephritis without lymphodepletion

Fan L, Fang F, Zhang C, Ye Z, Chen M, Tian Z, et al.

*Cellular & Molecular Immunology*

[10.1038/s41423-026-01489-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41423-026-01489-6)

Allogeneic NK cells engineered with NKG2D, high CD16 and membrane-bound IL-15/IL-15Rα, deployed with rituximab as the targeting arm.

This is an engager architecture turned inside out: rather than building the tumour-binding specificity into a soluble molecule, the specificity is supplied by an off-the-shelf therapeutic antibody and the effector cell is engineered to exploit it, with CD16 overexpression for antibody-dependent cytotoxicity, NKG2D for stress-ligand recognition on autoreactive B cells, and membrane-bound IL-15 for persistence without exogenous cytokine. The persistence data are the strongest part — up to ninety days in NOG mice at thirty-fold greater peak abundance than unmodified NK cells. In two patients with refractory mixed class IV plus V lupus nephritis, treatment without lymphodepletion produced rapid B cell depletion and proteinuria reductions of seventy-three and fifty-two per cent with no cytokine release syndrome or neurotoxicity. Two patients is an anecdote, not a result, and NKG2D ligand recognition is intrinsically less specific than a paratope, but the lymphodepletion-free framing is what makes it interesting for autoimmune indications.

DICE score: 3 — Shows a cytokine-armed, CD16-boosted effector cell can substitute for a soluble engager's effector arm, with first-in-human evidence that lymphodepletion can be skipped.

Clinical-stage (NCT06676631); University of Science and Technology of China with Shanghai NK CellTech.

### 8. Fixed-duration glofitamab in patients with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma: results from a phase 1/2 study

Morschhauser F, Falchi L, Carlo-Stella C, Bachy E, Crump M, Offner F, et al.

*HemaSphere*

[10.1002/hem3.70531](https://doi.org/10.1002/hem3.70531)

CD20 × CD3 2:1 format bispecific, given as time-limited monotherapy rather than treat-to-progression.

Fixed duration is a design question as much as a clinical one, because it asks whether a redirected T cell response can be installed and then left alone. In sixty-one patients at the thirty milligram target dose, overall response was eighty-five point two per cent and complete response seventy-seven per cent, with median duration of complete response of forty point two months after a median follow-up of twenty-nine point three months. Two observations matter for the engineering case: seventy-six point three per cent of patients had undetectable minimal residual disease by cycle three and it persisted past end of treatment, and T cell counts were not depressed by treatment, with immune recovery at twelve to eighteen months in those still in remission. Cytokine release syndrome occurred in sixty-two point three per cent of patients but was uniformly grade two or below, and neurotoxicity was rare. The implication is that the engager does not have to exhaust the effector pool it recruits.

DICE score: 3 — Mature fixed-duration data showing durable complete remissions and preserved T cell compartments, which is the clinical counterargument to continuous dosing of CD3 bispecifics.

Clinical-stage; Roche.

### 9. First-in-human, phase I study of clesitamig, a DLL3-targeted CD3×4-1BB trispecific T-cell engager in patients with advanced small-cell lung cancer and other neuroendocrine carcinomas

Yoshida T, Fowler S, Franjkovic I, Gochitashvili N, Lane C, Tanos T, et al.

*Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer*

[10.1136/jitc-2026-016578](https://doi.org/10.1136/jitc-2026-016578)

DLL3 × CD3 × 4-1BB trispecific T cell engager (clesitamig, RO7616789), with costimulation built into the molecule.

The design question behind this trial is whether a third specificity for 4-1BB can supply the costimulatory signal that two-chain DLL3 engagers lack, in a tumour type where DLL3-directed CD3 bispecifics have already shown activity but with limited durability. Folding costimulation into the engager rather than delivering it as a separate agonist is the architectural bet, and small-cell lung cancer and neuroendocrine carcinoma are a reasonable place to test it given the near-binary DLL3 expression. This entry records the publication of the first-in-human dose-escalation results, which land alongside a growing DLL3 trispecific field including alveltamig; the full abstract and efficacy and cytokine release syndrome figures were not retrievable from the publisher in this cycle, so the numbers are deliberately not summarised here and should be read from the paper before being quoted.

DICE score: 3 — First-in-human data for an engager carrying its own costimulatory arm in an indication where the two-specificity version is already clinically validated.

Clinical-stage; Roche, with a large multi-centre investigator group.

### 10. Outcomes associated with CD3xCD20 bispecific antibody therapies in relapsed/refractory transformed indolent lymphoma

Stephan P, Chohan KL, Redd RA, Dixon W, Zou A, Ahmed S, et al.

*Haematologica*

[10.3324/haematol.2026.301578](https://doi.org/10.3324/haematol.2026.301578)

CD20 × CD3 bispecifics (epcoritamab, glofitamab) in a histology where their activity had not been established.

Transformed indolent lymphoma is routinely folded into large B-cell lymphoma cohorts despite a different disease history, and this multi-centre retrospective tests whether that assumption holds for CD3×CD20 engagers. Across seventy-four patients with transformed disease and one hundred and sixty-six with de novo diffuse large B-cell lymphoma from nine academic centres, best overall response was fifty per cent versus fifty-one per cent and complete response thirty-one per cent versus thirty-four per cent, with twelve-month progression-free survival of twenty-five versus thirty-four per cent and a hazard ratio spanning one. The practical value is a negative finding with real consequences: antigen and effector biology appear not to differ enough between these histologies to justify separate expectations, which supports pooling them in engager trials. Retrospective, unrandomised, and with the confounding that follows from clinician-chosen therapy.

DICE score: 3 — Settles a real clinical uncertainty about whether CD3×CD20 engagers underperform in transformed indolent lymphoma; the answer appears to be no.

Collaborative US Bispecifics Consortium (CUBIC).

### 11. Innovative engineering strategies for T-cell engagers in solid tumors: from molecular design to clinical translation

Li J, Zhu M, Zhang Y, Gao Z, Qian C, Shan J

*Biomarker Research*

[10.1186/s40364-026-01042-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40364-026-01042-7)

Review covering miniaturisation, affinity optimisation, costimulatory integration, microenvironment-responsive prodrugs and escape-resistant architectures.

A reasonably current synthesis of the solid-tumour engager problem organised by engineering lever rather than by target, which makes it more useful than the usual target-by-target survey. Of note for practitioners, it carries sections on developability and chemistry, manufacturing and controls alongside the molecular design discussion, so the manufacturability constraints sit next to the architectures that create them. No new data, and the prodrug and costimulation sections necessarily overlap with primary papers in this same digest.

DICE score: 2 — Competent and well-organised review of engager engineering for solid tumours, useful as a reference map rather than a new contribution.

Chongqing University.

### 12. Biomarkers and molecular determinants of T-cell engager response, resistance and toxicity

Li W, Wu Y, Cheng M, Wu S, Liu M, Zou L

*Biochemical Pharmacology*

[10.1016/j.bcp.2026.118653](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bcp.2026.118653)

Review across host T cell fitness, molecular design and pharmacology, antigen ecology, the tumour immune ecosystem and toxicity risk.

The framing worth borrowing is that target positivity is not a selection criterion, and that productive engagement needs competent T cells, adequate active exposure at the tumour, persistent accessible antigen, trafficking, and an activation threshold that is calibrated rather than maximal. The review is honest about the state of the evidence: most reported markers are prognostic, pharmacodynamic, or useful for classifying resistance after the fact, and almost none are validated predictors of benefit. Design-relevant sections connect format, pharmacokinetics, activation threshold and synapse geometry to the cytotoxicity-versus-inflammation balance, which is the trade-off every engager programme is implicitly tuning.

DICE score: 2 — Useful consolidation of what engager biomarkers can and cannot currently support, with a clear-eyed account of how little is predictive.

West China Hospital, Sichuan University.

## Notable Preprints

### 1. Biomolecular design through latent reasoning with RFD4-Proteina

Corley N, Didi K, Kazman MW, Brent RI, Bauer MS, Biswas R, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.12.766037](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.12.766037)

Generative design model over a shared latent space spanning standard and modified amino acids, sugars, DNA and RNA, with force-field supervision.

The headline for binder engineering is that one model now covers side-chain packing, sequence design, conformational sampling and refinement at or above specialist state of the art, while adding two forms of test-time compute: recurrent refinement that allocates more computation where the problem is harder, and guided search under arbitrary energetic or geometric constraints. Experimentally the authors report specific and deliberately cross-reactive binders to therapeutically relevant targets, binders that read phosphorylation state, control of glycosylation, and de novo enzymes including a first proof of concept for pharmaceutical ingredient synthesis. For multispecific design the cross-reactive-binder and constraint-guided-search capabilities are the directly relevant ones, since a trispecific is largely a constrained geometry problem over three interfaces.

DICE score: 5 — A single physically grounded design model that handles modified residues, glycans and nucleic acids with constraint-guided search, which is the capability multispecific engineering has been missing.

Baker lab, Institute for Protein Design, University of Washington.

### 2. VINCIT: a virus-incorporated biomaterial niche enables spatially controlled in vivo CAR T cell engineering

Goodier KD, Zhu Y, Wei C, Jiang Z, Jiang Y, Lu X, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.16.767176](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.16.767176)

Injectable nanofiber hydrogel composite that retains viral vector locally and recruits endogenous immune cells for in situ transduction.

In vivo cell engineering is the main competitor to soluble engagers for off-the-shelf redirection, and its central problem is that systemic vector delivery hits the wrong cells. This approach makes the delivery site into a recruitment niche: the gel holds adeno-associated or lentiviral vector in place, endogenous cells come to it, transduced T cells then leave for the circulation. Transgene expression was spatially confined relative to intravenous delivery and transduction was far better than free subcutaneous virus, and a single administration extended median survival from thirty-two to one hundred and ten days in a humanised lymphoma model. The mechanistic finding that neutrophil depletion sharply reduces recruitment and subsequent programming is the part that will decide whether this generalises, since it means the niche depends on an inflammatory response that may vary between patients.

DICE score: 4 — Turns the injection site into the engineering compartment, which addresses the specificity problem that limits in vivo vector-delivered cell therapy.

Johns Hopkins University, Mao group.

### 3. Chimeric antigen receptor T cell therapy targeting CD94 for cytotoxic T/NK cell malignancies

Le CC, Weng J, Liu J, Aletti FM, Cherng X, Tang Y, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.12.765663](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.12.765663)

CD94 as a post-thymic target antigen, with a truncated BCMA safety switch and anti-fratricide behaviour.

Three separable contributions here, all transferable to engager design. The target is new and chosen on the right criterion — restricted normal-tissue and immune-cell expression but enrichment on cytotoxic T and NK malignancies, which avoids the prolonged T cell aplasia that pan-T targeting causes. The safety switch is a truncated BCMA tag that lets an existing anti-BCMA engager or antibody-drug conjugate serve as the off-switch, which is an elegant reuse of approved molecules as a control layer. And super-resolution imaging shows the CAR sequesters CD94 intracellularly, which is why the construct does not kill itself; that observation is a direct warning for any engager aimed at an antigen the effector cell also carries.

DICE score: 3 — A credible new target antigen for a poorly served malignancy, plus a safety-switch design that repurposes existing BCMA-directed drugs as the control mechanism.

MD Anderson Cancer Center, Neelapu group.

### 4. From adalimumab to orally administered secretory IgA: preclinical characterization of ABC-301

Brousse M, Pintat A, Lejeune B, Favre-Bulle O, Herbert J-M, Cuvillier A

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.16.766909](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.16.766909)

Adalimumab variable regions reformatted into a recombinant human secretory IgA1, dosed orally.

Format reformatting is usually about half-life or valency; this is about route and about immunogenicity. Moving the same paratopes into a secretory IgA1 scaffold gave oral activity in dextran sulfate sodium colitis with iodine-125 biodistribution confined largely to gastrointestinal compartments, and a whole-molecule neutralisation half-maximal inhibitory concentration about three point three-fold lower than adalimumab, which shrinks to roughly one point six-fold once normalised for binding-site count — so most of the gain is valency, honestly reported. The more striking result is reduced functional inhibition by seven patient-derived anti-adalimumab antibody preparations, meaning a scaffold change can partially escape pre-existing anti-drug antibodies against the same paratopes. For engagers facing immunogenicity attrition, that is a lever worth noting even though sIgA's size and complexity make it an unlikely engager chassis.

DICE score: 3 — Demonstrates that re-scaffolding a known paratope changes both route of administration and susceptibility to existing anti-drug antibodies, with the valency contribution properly separated out.

ABCELY.

## Weekly Themes

The dominant pattern this week is conditionality: four of the strongest molecules work by withholding a signal until local conditions permit it. The logic-gated DVD-3E buries both its CD3 arm and its IL-2 moiety behind a protease gate and an affinity penalty; AMP-410 gates 4-1BB agonism on VEGF binding, substituting a secreted factor for a surface antigen as the localisation cue; the outer membrane vesicle nanoengager gates engager release on matrix metalloproteinase 2; and clesitamig takes the complementary route of hard-wiring costimulation into a third specificity rather than gating it. Alongside this runs a quieter argument about where selectivity actually comes from. The tetravalent CD7 conjugate shows that avidity-based selectivity can be defeated by dense antigen patches and proposes internalisation kinetics instead, while etentamig's clinical data make the case that putting avidity on the tumour arm and detuning CD3 is what buys a tolerable schedule — two papers converging on the view that affinity and valency are not interchangeable knobs. Binding-domain provenance is a third thread: TriPRIL's trimerised APRIL and the CD94 CAR's choice of a post-thymic antigen both reject the reflex of pairing two scFvs against the obvious lineage marker, and ABC-301 shows a scaffold swap can change immunogenicity against unchanged paratopes. Finally, the in vivo delivery route keeps gaining ground on soluble molecules, with VINCIT localising vector in an injectable niche and the trifunctional NK product dispensing with lymphodepletion entirely — and over all of it, RFD4-Proteina is the week's reminder that the design bottleneck for multispecifics is becoming geometric and computational rather than experimental.
