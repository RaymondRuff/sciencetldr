## Top Papers

### 1. Computer-guided dosing of multi-specific T-cell engagers

Kendell I, McShinsky R, Modzelewska K, Naatz L, Stark A, Bearss D, et al.

*Communications Biology*

[10.1038/s42003-026-10602-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10602-4)

CD19/CD20/CD38 × CD3 modular split-antibody T-cell engager (MATCH platform) with a computational composition/dosing framework (COMPASS).

Rather than a single fixed bispecific, MATCH is a modular "split-antibody" TCE in which separate targeting modules are combined on demand, and COMPASS is a lab-in-the-loop computational framework that reads out a patient's T-cell count, tumor burden, and CD19/CD20/CD38 antigen expression to predict the multi-specific composition and dose that maximizes killing while sparing T cells. The framework predicted effective formulations for CLL patient PBMCs and prospectively designed a mixture that improved survival in a human lymphoma xenograft. It reframes multispecific engager design from "one molecule for all patients" to a composable, model-guided cocktail tuned per patient.

DICE score: 4 — A genuinely differentiated modular split-antibody engager architecture coupled to a predictive computational design/dosing engine, offering a transferable blueprint for patient-specific multispecific targeting.

University of Utah (Owen/Gambles, Huntsman Cancer Institute).

### 2. Bispecific antibody AP402 targets p95HER2 and induces costimulation through CD137 to improve efficacy for HER2-positive tumors

Huang PL, Hsieh HT, Kan HT, Hsu CH, Huang KM, Chen HY, et al.

*Molecular Medicine*

[10.1186/s10020-026-01564-8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s10020-026-01564-8)

p95HER2 × CD137 (4-1BB) costimulatory bispecific antibody.

AP402 is built around a differentiated targeting axis: it binds the truncated p95HER2 variant that lacks the trastuzumab epitope and marks resistant, aggressive tumors, while its CD137 arm delivers T-cell costimulation only in a p95HER2-binding-dependent (tumor-conditional) manner. The construct drove ADCC against both p95HER2 and full-length HER2 cells, produced antigen-dependent IFN-γ, and shrank tumors in humanized p95HER2-transgenic and PDX models, with cytokine-release and cynomolgus toxicology suggesting a low adverse-event risk. Conditional 4-1BB agonism gated on a tumor-restricted escape antigen is a clean answer to both the safety liability of systemic costimulation and HER2-therapy resistance.

DICE score: 4 — A differentiated conditional-costimulatory bispecific against a validated escape antigen (p95HER2), with a full preclinical efficacy and primate-safety package that positions it for the clinic.

AP Biosciences (Taipei); clinical-stage candidate.

### 3. Englumafusp alfa plus glofitamab in B cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma: a phase 1 trial

Hutchings M, Dickinson M, Gritti G, Carlo-Stella C, Townsend W, Bosch F, et al.

*Nature Medicine*

[10.1038/s41591-026-04533-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04533-0)

CD19-4-1BBL costimulatory bispecific (englumafusp alfa) combined with a CD20 × CD3 T-cell engager (glofitamab).

This 134-patient phase 1 study is the clearest clinical test to date of the "signal 2 on top of the engager" design thesis: pairing a tumor-anchored 4-1BBL costimulatory bispecific with an approved CD20×CD3 TCE. The maximum tolerated dose of englumafusp alfa was not reached, safety mirrored glofitamab monotherapy, and pharmacodynamics confirmed the intended costimulatory mode of action, with overall/complete response rates of 68.7%/56.6% in the aggressive-B-NHL subgroup (73.2%/65.9% in CAR-naïve patients). It provides human validation that a separately dosed costimulatory engager can augment a T-cell engager without adding a toxicity penalty.

DICE score: 4 — Large phase 1 clinical validation of the costimulatory-engager-plus-TCE combination concept, with encouraging efficacy and a clean safety read that is likely to shape next-generation engager regimen design.

Roche/multicenter; clinical-stage (NCT04077723).

### 4. Functional immune profiling translates T cell dynamics into predictive biomarkers for myeloma immunotherapy

Herzberg F, Lu F, Korenkov M, Fregona V, Didoss C, Heisterkamp N, et al.

*Leukemia*

[10.1038/s41375-026-03047-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41375-026-03047-5)

BCMA × CD3 and GPRC5D × CD3 engagers (teclistamab, talquetamab) — ex vivo immunological-synapse readout.

Using patient bone-marrow mononuclear cells and multiplex imaging, the authors quantify engager-driven plasma-cell lysis, T-cell expansion, morphology, and spatial T-cell–plasma-cell engagement at single-cell resolution, resolving a morphologic activation trajectory that distinguishes productive from abortive immunological synapses. Three functional phenotypes (non-responder, cytotoxic, cytotoxic-expansive) stratified real clinical response and time on therapy. For engager design this supplies a mechanistic, synapse-level potency assay that connects molecular engagement quality to patient outcomes.

DICE score: 3 — A useful mechanistic and biomarker advance that operationalizes immunological-synapse quality as a functional readout of engager activity and clinical response.

Charité/MDC Berlin.

### 5. Blinatumomab consolidation for high-risk Ph− B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia: the GRAALL-2014/B-QUEST study

Boissel N, Huguet F, Leguay T, Hunault M, Kim R, Hicheri Y, et al.

*Blood*

[10.1182/blood.2026033309](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2026033309)

CD19 × CD3 tandem-scFv T-cell engager (blinatumomab) in frontline high-risk B-ALL.

In this phase 2 substudy, adding blinatumomab consolidation/maintenance to high-risk (KMT2A-r, IKZF1 deletion, or MRD ≥10⁻⁴) B-ALL improved MRD clearance and, at 5 years, cut cumulative relapse (23% vs 49%) and raised disease-free (68% vs 42%) and overall survival (79% vs 60%) versus historical controls. Notably, no additional DFS benefit accrued specifically to patients who went on to transplant. The data reinforce the archetypal CD19×CD3 engager as a frontline backbone and pressure-test where transplant still adds value.

DICE score: 3 — Important frontline clinical efficacy data for the field's prototype T-cell engager, with practice-relevant implications for the role of transplant.

GRAALL cooperative group; clinical (NCT03709719).

### 6. Albumin binding improves the pharmacokinetics and therapeutic efficacy of ¹⁷⁷Lu-labeled HER2 Fab radioconjugate

Liu P, Pang Y, Li C, Hua Y, Zhen W, Zhao T, et al.

*Science Advances*

[10.1126/sciadv.aee4052](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aee4052)

HER2 Fab fused to an albumin-binding domain (ABD) for half-life extension of an antibody fragment.

The work is a clean demonstration that an albumin-binding domain can rebalance the size/PK trade-off of a small binding fragment: ¹⁷⁷Lu-DOTA-Fab-ABD keeps a Fab's rapid tumor penetration while borrowing albumin's long circulation, improving tumor dosimetry and sharply lowering hematologic toxicity versus a full-length antibody. Although demonstrated as a radioconjugate, the ABD-fragment format is a generalizable half-life-extension chassis directly relevant to short-lived engagers that otherwise require continuous infusion.

DICE score: 3 — A transferable fragment half-life-extension engineering strategy, well-characterized in vivo, with clear applicability to the PK liabilities of small engager formats.

National University of Singapore / Chen lab.

### 7. Developability engineering of scFvs enables robust CAR function under transient mRNA expression

Mangolini M, Ramon A, Li B, Souster E, Hayre J, Karattil R, et al.

*Scientific Reports*

[10.1038/s41598-026-60295-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-60295-2)

scFv binding-domain developability engineering (FMC63, 14G2a, MGA271) for mRNA-delivered constructs.

Using a computational developability- and humanness-guided workflow, the authors show that the intrinsic biophysical quality of an scFv governs surface expression and function when expression is transient, as with mRNA-LNP delivery: stabilizing the aggregation-prone 14G2a scFv markedly boosted expression and cytotoxicity, while reducing MGA271 non-specific interactions cut off-target killing even at lower monovalent affinity. Because engagers share the same scFv building blocks and the field is moving toward in vivo/mRNA-encoded formats, these binder-quality rules transfer directly to engager arm design.

DICE score: 3 — A practical, generalizable methodological contribution linking computational scFv developability to functional output, increasingly relevant as engagers move to transient-expression delivery.

LabGenius / University of Cambridge (Sormanni).

### 8. Targeted mRNA Delivery Using Bispecific Antibody-Lipid Nanoparticle Complexes

Hickey JC, Roach T, Cassaidy B, Hergesheimer R, Franklin N, Morin J, et al.

*Molecular Pharmaceutics*

[10.1021/acs.molpharmaceut.6c00239](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.molpharmaceut.6c00239)

Anti-PEG × cell-marker bispecific antibody (including anti-CD3) as a non-covalent LNP-targeting adaptor.

The bispecific here is repurposed as a modular "clamp": one arm grabs the PEG on an LNP surface and the other a chosen cell-surface marker, redirecting mRNA-LNP uptake to defined cell types without any chemical conjugation. The authors demonstrate targeted delivery to CD3-expressing lymphoblasts and TfR1⁺ cells in vitro and in vivo, and characterize endosomal-escape kinetics as a function of bispecific loading. It is an inventive use of bispecific format engineering to solve extrahepatic, T-cell-directed delivery — a plug-and-play targeting layer relevant to in vivo engager and CAR generation.

DICE score: 3 — A novel modular application of the bispecific format as a conjugation-free cell-targeting adaptor for mRNA delivery, with demonstrated T-cell targeting in vivo.

Pfizer (Chaparro-Riggers group).

### 9. Safety and clinical outcomes of a first-in-human trial of point-of-care manufactured trispecific CAR T cells targeting CD19, CD20, and CD22

Vasu S, Denlinger N, Song NJ, Elsberry D, Zhao Q, Yu L, et al.

*Blood Cancer Discovery*

[10.1158/2643-3230.BCD-26-0186](https://doi.org/10.1158/2643-3230.BCD-26-0186)

Trispecific CD19/CD20/CD22 CAR with an OX40 costimulatory domain, point-of-care manufactured.

To pre-empt single-antigen escape, this first-in-human trial deployed a single trispecific CAR covering CD19, CD20, and CD22 with OX40 costimulation, manufactured at the point of care with a median 7-day vein-to-vein time. Across 16 heavily pretreated patients there was no severe CRS or neurotoxicity, a 50% overall response rate (83% CR in lymphoma), and 61% one-year survival, with apheresis T-cell exhaustion — not dose — tracking with progression. The multi-antigen construct design and its clean tolerability are directly instructive for how many targets an immune-redirecting molecule needs to durably outrun antigen loss.

DICE score: 3 — Important first-in-human data for a multi-antigen (tri-targeted) redirecting construct designed against escape, with implications for engager target-selection strategy.

Ohio State University (de Lima/Alinari); clinical.

### 10. Leveraging protein language model for maturation of high-affinity peptide ligand in the purification of an adeno-associated virus vector

Zhao Y, Wang F, Dong X, Jia G, Yu L, Sun Y, et al.

*Journal of Chromatography A*

[10.1016/j.chroma.2026.467271](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chroma.2026.467271)

Protein language model (ESM3)–guided de novo design and affinity maturation of a peptide binder.

The study couples de novo peptide design with the multimodal ESM3 protein language model to drive iterative, docking-scored affinity maturation, converging on a serotype-specific AAV2-capsid binder after three rounds. Although the target is a purification ligand, the transferable contribution is the language-model-in-the-loop maturation workflow — generate, score by predicted structure/interface, iterate — which is a lightweight route to improving affinity and specificity of engager binding arms.

DICE score: 3 — A useful demonstration of a protein-language-model-guided affinity-maturation loop that is method-transferable to engager binder engineering, tempered by a non-engager target and modest (µM) final affinity.

Tianjin University.

## Notable Preprints

### 11. Integrating activation-induced costimulation and cytokine signals enhance TCR-based cell therapies

Narula M, Englisch J, Ou C, Honda T, San Sebastian IdlI, Arnett AB, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.07.12.738084](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.12.738084)

Dual stimulatory receptor (DSR): 4-1BBL ectodomain fused to the thrombopoietin-receptor (cMPL) endodomain, arming soluble-TCE- and TCR-redirected T cells.

DSR senses the 4-1BB transiently upregulated on TCR- or engager-activated T cells and converts it into simultaneous signal 2 (costimulation) and signal 3 (cMPL-driven STAT3/5 cytokine signaling), improving T-cell expansion, effector persistence, and in vivo antitumor activity of cells redirected by soluble T-cell engagers. It directly targets the durability gap that limits engager-redirected T cells with a single modular receptor.

DICE score: 3 — A modular receptor that boosts persistence of soluble-engager-redirected T cells, addressing a core engager limitation; preclinical and awaiting peer review.

Baylor College of Medicine (Watanabe).

### 12. The CD19-4-1BBL antibody fusion protein unleashes the immune system against high-risk chronic lymphocytic leukemia

Khare P, Zhang R, Ivan C, Schneider S, Banerjee P, Sobhani N, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.07.09.737515](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.09.737515)

CD19 × 4-1BBL costimulatory bispecific antibody-fusion protein.

Extending the CD19-4-1BBL costimulatory engager beyond its known T-cell-cytotoxic role, this work shows it simultaneously reprograms the immunosuppressive myeloid/MDSC/Treg compartment and expands memory CD8⁺ clonotypes, translating into a survival benefit in patient-derived CLL xenografts — most strongly in high-risk/progressive disease. It broadens the design rationale for 4-1BB-arming engagers to include remodeling of the suppressive microenvironment.

DICE score: 3 — Mechanistic evidence that a costimulatory bispecific acts on more than T cells, informing costimulatory-engager design for immunosuppressed tumors; preprint.

MD Anderson Cancer Center (Bertilaccio).

### 13. Machine Learning-Guided Engineering of High-Affinity Cross-Reactive Antibodies with Minimal Mutations

Hugo D, Grindel AL, Thenier F, Pluchart C, Munch M, Oliveira C, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.07.15.738714](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.15.738714)

Deep mutational scanning + deep learning to build human/mouse cross-reactive antibody variants (anti-PD-L1).

Starting from a human anti-PD-L1 antibody with weak murine cross-reactivity, the authors use DMS-trained deep learning to find minimal (4–5) substitution sets that confer full human/mouse cross-reactivity — versus 13–15 by naïve recombination — while preserving PD-1/PD-L1 blockade and in vivo efficacy. Minimizing mutational burden to gain species cross-reactivity is a practical developability lever for making engager arms testable in immunocompetent preclinical models.

DICE score: 3 — A transferable ML-guided affinity/cross-reactivity engineering method with minimal-mutation developability benefits directly applicable to engager binder design; preprint.

CEA (Nozach).

### 14. De novo design of selective kinase modulators

Bauer MS, Lee GR, Coventry B, Klupt KA, Fernandez-Escamilla AM, Kumar S, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.07.10.737808](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.10.737808)

De novo genetically encoded miniprotein binders that stabilize specific conformational states of a target.

From 96 designs against focal adhesion kinase, 33 modulated activity, yielding low-nanomolar inhibitors and activators that worked in living cells, and the FAK inhibitors were retargeted to Src by redesign. The transferable idea for engagers is conformation-selective de novo binders against highly conserved surfaces — a route to specificity that affinity alone cannot buy when designing novel targeting arms.

DICE score: 3 — A methodological advance in conformation-selective de novo binder design from the Baker lab, transferable to engineering selective engager targeting domains; preprint.

Institute for Protein Design, University of Washington (Baker).

### 15. Potent substoichiometric inhibition of alpha-synuclein aggregation by de novo oligomer-binding proteins

Zhang Y, Han HL, Ortigosa-Pascual L, Miles UZ, Snow F, Tu D, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.07.13.738012](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.13.738012)

De novo single-chain binders against a disordered/extended-strand epitope, with partial-diffusion affinity maturation.

Deep-learning design produced compact single-chain binders that engage the intrinsically disordered NAC domain of α-synuclein by stabilizing its extended β-strand, and a single round of partial diffusion matured the best binder to 1.94 nM with no cross-reactivity to tau, amylin, or amyloid-β. The methods — designing against a conformationally elusive epitope and using partial diffusion for rapid affinity maturation — are a template for building specific engager arms against otherwise "undruggable" targets.

DICE score: 2 — Elegant de novo binder design and affinity-maturation methodology against a hard epitope, though the disease target is distant from engager applications; preprint.

University of Cambridge (Knowles) with IPD collaborators.

## Weekly Themes

This week's through-line is **modularity and computation reshaping how engagers are designed and dosed**. The standout is a split-antibody T-cell engager paired with a predictive framework (MATCH/COMPASS) that composes patient-specific multispecific cocktails — a shift from fixed molecules toward composable, model-guided targeting that echoes the trispecific multi-antigen logic appearing on the clinical side (CD19/20/22 CAR) as a hedge against antigen escape. **Costimulation was the second dominant motif:** a tumor-conditional p95HER2×CD137 bispecific, human phase 1 validation of a CD19-4-1BBL costimulatory bispecific layered onto glofitamab, mechanistic CLL data on the same 4-1BBL fusion, and a modular DSR receptor delivering synchronized signals 2 and 3 — collectively signaling that the field increasingly treats durable T-cell "signal 2/3" support as a co-equal design axis alongside the redirecting bispecific itself. A steady **AI/de novo protein-design current** ran underneath (ESM3-guided affinity maturation, DMS+deep-learning minimal-mutation cross-reactivity, conformation-selective de novo miniproteins, partial-diffusion maturation), all offering transferable routes to specific, developable binding arms. Finally, several papers targeted the unglamorous but decisive **developability and delivery** layer — scFv biophysical quality for transient/mRNA expression, albumin-binding half-life extension of fragments, and bispecific-clamped LNPs for cell-targeted mRNA — reflecting a field maturing toward in vivo-generated and manufacturability-robust engager formats.
