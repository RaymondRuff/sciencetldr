## Top Papers

### 1. Covalently PD-L1 Anchoring Drives Bispecific Nanostructure Assembly for Spatial Control of T Cell Recruitment and Activation

Zhang F, Dong Y, Liu K, Hu H, Chang H, Zuo J, Ma X, Xu J, et al.

*Angewandte Chemie (International ed. in English)*

[10.1002/anie.5894316](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.5894316)

PD-L1-anchored self-assembled surface nanostructure acting as a synthetic, BiTE-mimetic T-cell engager.

Instead of building a recombinant engager, the authors use a "localized oxidation–covalent assembly" strategy: tumor-surface PD-L1 glycans are selectively oxidized to aldehydes, then bioorthogonally elaborated in situ into artificial topological nanostructures that both block the PD-1/PD-L1 axis and transmembrane-bridge T cells to the tumor cell, functionally mimicking a BiTE. Because the engager is assembled directly on the target-cell surface from a checkpoint ligand, T-cell recruitment and activation become spatially constrained to PD-L1⁺ cells, giving programmable, location-restricted engagement without a soluble bispecific protein. It reframes engager construction as a cell-surface chemical-assembly problem rather than a protein-format problem.

DICE score: 4 — A genuinely novel, protein-free route to T-cell engagement that self-assembles a BiTE-mimetic on the tumor surface from an endogenous checkpoint ligand, offering built-in spatial selectivity and a new design axis for the engager field.

Zhengzhou University, College of Pharmacy.

### 2. Dual-modular-nanobody CAR-T cell technical platform against the solid tumor microenvironment

Li Y, Wang X, Zhang S, Peng T, Gong Z, Jiang H, Huang X, Xie S, et al.

*Journal of Hematology & Oncology*

[10.1186/s13045-026-01821-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13045-026-01821-7)

FAP-targeting nanobody CAR with local secretion of an anti-CTLA-4 VHH (dual-modular nanobody, DMN).

The platform couples a fibroblast-activation-protein (FAP) nanobody CAR to in-tumor release of an anti-CTLA-4 single-domain antibody, so checkpoint blockade is delivered locally in the stroma rather than systemically, decoupling efficacy from the severe toxicity of systemic CTLA-4 antagonism. The all-nanobody, modular design gave potent antigen-specific cytotoxicity with reduced exhaustion in vitro, tumor regression and increased T-cell infiltration in xenografts, and — in a first-in-human intrathecal infusion for refractory glioblastoma — disease stabilization with elevated CSF cytokines and a favorable safety profile. The local-payload nanobody architecture and TME-remodeling logic transfer directly to engager and armored-cell designs targeting stroma.

DICE score: 4 — A differentiated all-nanobody dual-module architecture that localizes checkpoint blockade to the tumor stroma, with preclinical validation plus early human proof-of-concept, addressing a central solid-tumor bottleneck.

Guangxi Key Laboratory of Nanobody Research, Guangxi Medical University.

### 3. EGFRxCD16 bispecific antibodies orchestrate superior NK cell-mediated lysis of ovarian cancer and NSCLC cell lines in combination with oncolytic viruses

Gahbauer S, Poiret T, et al.

*Cancer Immunology, Immunotherapy*

[10.1007/s00262-026-04470-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00262-026-04470-4)

EGFR × CD16 bispecific NK-cell engager combined with GM-CSF- and ICOSL-armed oncolytic viruses.

The study pairs an EGFR × CD16 bispecific NK-cell engager with oncolytic viruses (ONCOS-102/ONCOS-204) engineered to express GM-CSF or ICOSL, showing that the bispecific is the dominant driver of NK activation, degranulation, and cytotoxicity while OV preconditioning further sensitizes tumor targets. The combination was most potent against ovarian and EGFR-mutant lung lines, supporting a rational two-agent strategy in which viral remodeling of the tumor primes engager-redirected NK killing. The work is a useful format-plus-modality combination for solid tumors where NK engagers alone underperform.

DICE score: 3 — A well-characterized demonstration that oncolytic-virus preconditioning potentiates an NK-cell engager in solid-tumor models, a translationally relevant combination but built on an existing bispecific format.

Karolinska Institutet, Stockholm.

### 4. Real-World Outcomes with BCMA- and GPRC5D-Targeting Bispecific Antibodies in Plasma Cell Leukemia

Gaballa MR, Julian K, Afrough A, Hansen DK, Goel U, De Menezes Silva Corraes A, Dima D, Rana MS, et al.

*Blood Advances*

[10.1182/bloodadvances.2026020826](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2026020826)

BCMA × CD3 (teclistamab, elranatamab) and GPRC5D × CD3 (talquetamab) bispecific engagers.

This 122-patient, 15-center retrospective study extends T-cell-redirecting bispecifics to plasma cell leukemia, a high-risk population routinely excluded from pivotal engager trials. Across a heavily pretreated cohort, the GPRC5D × CD3 engager talquetamab delivered markedly superior response and survival (ORR 55–61%; talquetamab mPFS/mOS 5.5/11.5 months as a line of therapy) versus BCMA-directed engagers, with mostly low-grade CRS and neurotoxicity. The data provide the first sizeable real-world signal that antigen choice (GPRC5D vs BCMA) meaningfully shapes engager efficacy in aggressive disease.

DICE score: 3 — Important real-world clinical data in a trial-excluded population showing a clear antigen-dependent efficacy gap between GPRC5D- and BCMA-directed engagers, with direct implications for target selection.

Clinical-stage; multicenter US academic consortium (MD Anderson and others).

### 5. Short ramp-up glofitamab halves mortality risk after anti-CD19 CAR T-cell therapy failure in patients with diffuse large B-cell lymphoma: final results of the LYSA BiCAR phase 2 trial

Cartron G, Chaillol I, Houot R, Al Tabaa Y, Le Bras F, Ysebaert L, Choquet S, Jardin F, et al.

*Journal of Hematology & Oncology*

[10.1186/s13045-026-01816-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13045-026-01816-4)

CD20 × CD3 bispecific engager (glofitamab) with accelerated step-up dosing.

The final BiCAR analysis reports that short-ramp-up glofitamab given at first relapse/progression after anti-CD19 CAR-T failure nearly halved the risk of death versus a propensity-weighted external control of non-bispecific salvage (median OS 19.6 vs 7.6 months; HR 0.49). Beyond the survival result, the accelerated 8-day step-up to 30 mg is a practical dosing-schedule refinement for a setting with no standard of care. The study strengthens the case for sequencing a CD20 × CD3 engager immediately after CAR-T failure.

DICE score: 3 — Robust comparative-effectiveness clinical data positioning a CD20 × CD3 engager as a preferred option after CAR-T failure, with an actionable accelerated ramp-up schedule.

Clinical-stage; LYSA cooperative group, France (NCT04703686).

### 6. Efficacy and Tolerability of Zenocutuzumab in Advanced NRG1 Fusion-Positive Cholangiocarcinoma: Results From the eNRGy Phase II Trial

Cleary JM, Springfeld C, Arnold D, Hollebecque A, Gbolahan O, Nagasaka M, Siena S, Opdam F, et al.

*Journal of Clinical Oncology*

[10.1200/JCO-25-03128](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-03128)

HER2 × HER3 bispecific antibody (zenocutuzumab) using a "Dock & Block" NRG1-signaling mechanism.

In 22 patients with NRG1 fusion-positive cholangiocarcinoma — a setting with no approved targeted therapy — the HER2 × HER3 bispecific zenocutuzumab produced a 36.8% ORR with a median 7.4-month duration of response and manageable toxicity. Mechanistically, the bispecific docks on HER2 and blocks NRG1–HER3 engagement, illustrating how bispecific geometry can be used to interrupt a specific ligand-driven signaling axis rather than simply co-engaging two antigens. It expands the actionable indication set for a differentiated non-T-cell bispecific format.

DICE score: 3 — Meaningful clinical activity for a mechanistically distinctive HER2 × HER3 signaling-blocking bispecific in a biomarker-defined, unmet-need tumor, reinforcing format-driven mechanism design.

Clinical-stage; Dana-Farber Cancer Institute and international eNRGy consortium.

### 7. Site-specific trastuzumab Fab-DM1 via butelase-1 with ABD fusion for potent HER2-positive tumor control

Han Y, Guo Q, Cao H, Qin T, Zhang X, Dong Q, Huang Y, Mao Y, et al.

*Medical Oncology*

[10.1007/s12032-026-03317-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12032-026-03317-3)

Compact anti-HER2 Fab–DM1 conjugate, enzymatically (butelase-1) site-specific, with albumin-binding-domain half-life extension.

To overcome poor tumor penetration of IgG-format conjugates and heterogeneity from stochastic conjugation, the authors build a small Fab-based construct with a defined drug-to-antibody ratio installed enzymatically by the peptide ligase butelase-1, and restore serum persistence by genetically fusing an albumin-binding domain. The three engineering levers demonstrated here — compact Fab scaffold, site-specific enzymatic payload/domain ligation, and ABD-mediated half-life extension — are directly portable to multispecific and engager assembly, where homogeneous, defined-stoichiometry linkage and pharmacokinetic tuning are recurring problems.

DICE score: 3 — A transferable enzymatic site-specific conjugation plus albumin-binding half-life strategy on a compact Fab scaffold, addressing homogeneity and PK bottlenecks common to engager construction.

Zhengzhou University, Longhu Laboratory of Advanced Immunology.

### 8. AI-Driven Design Platforms of Next-Generation Antibody Therapeutics

Wang Y, Saba A, Ran Y, Shehzadi K, Zhang Q, Liang J, Yu M, et al.

*Topics in Current Chemistry*

[10.1007/s41061-026-00554-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41061-026-00554-y)

Review — machine-learning/deep-learning platforms for antibody structure, affinity, and developability design.

This review maps how generative models (GANs), reinforcement learning, and multi-omics integration are moving antibody discovery from physics-based CADD toward data-driven prediction of structure, binding affinity, and pharmacological/biophysical properties. For engager engineering it is a useful orientation to the tooling now available for designing and de-risking binding arms, though it consolidates rather than extends the state of the art.

DICE score: 2 — A timely but non-experimental synthesis of AI platforms for antibody design, useful as a reference for engager arm engineering without new data.

Ministry of Industry and Information Technology molecular pharmaceutics lab.

### 9. Human single domain antibody-based CAR-T cells targeting BAFF-R demonstrate promising preclinical activity in B-cell malignancies

Zhang C, Luo Q, Wu S, Xu L, Wei Q, Zhang Y, Guan C, Dong X, et al.

*BMC Cancer*

[10.1186/s12885-026-16352-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-026-16352-0)

Fully human single heavy-chain variable domain (VHH-only) anti-BAFF-R CAR.

Replacing the bulky scFv binder with a fully human heavy-chain-only variable domain, the authors generate compact anti-BAFF-R CARs (from phage display against a target that can escape CD19 loss) that show specific activation and potent cytotoxicity, with two lead clones outperforming on cytotoxicity and expansion. The demonstration that a small, fully human single-domain binder preserves potency against BAFF-R is relevant to shrinking and humanizing the tumor-binding arms of engagers and multispecifics.

DICE score: 2 — A useful but incremental single-domain binder swap validating compact fully human VHH targeting of BAFF-R, with preclinical-only data.

Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital, Nanjing University.

### 10. Nanobodies against Plasmodium adhesins that block receptor engagement and malaria parasite invasion

D Sa J, Chmielewski J, Adair A, Tan LL, Chan LJ, Krauss L, Zeglinski K, Gouil Q, et al.

*Biochemical Journal*

[10.1042/BCJ20260354](https://doi.org/10.1042/BCJ20260354)

Anti-PfRh5/PfRh4/PvRBP2b nanobodies plus computationally stabilized, conserved adhesin scaffold variants.

Beyond generating high-affinity, invasion-blocking nanobodies across multiple epitopes of the RBL adhesin family, the authors use computational design to build stabilized PfRh4 scaffold variants with improved expression that retain binding to nanobodies and the human receptor CR1. The finding that inhibitory nanobodies converge on a conserved structural scaffold, plus the computational stabilization workflow, is a transferable template for generating and engineering single-domain binders against structurally conserved target families.

DICE score: 2 — Solid single-domain binder discovery with a computational scaffold-stabilization method that is methodologically transferable, though outside the core engager application.

Walter and Eliza Hall Institute of Medical Research.

### 11. Bispecific Antibodies in Solid Tumors: Mechanistic Insights, Clinical Advances, and Future Directions

Almosa R, Mendoza MM, Tang TY, Stephen B

*Journal of Immunotherapy and Precision Oncology*

[10.36401/JIPO-25-36](https://doi.org/10.36401/JIPO-25-36)

Review — Fc-based vs fragment-based bispecific formats and the eight approved solid-tumor bispecifics.

The review organizes bispecific antibodies by function (immune-cell engager, checkpoint modulator, signaling inhibitor) and by format (Fc-based vs fragment-based), tying format choice to half-life, stability, and tissue penetration, and catalogs the eight solid-tumor approvals (amivantamab, tebentafusp, cadonilimab, tarlatamab, ivonescimab, zanidatamab, zenocutuzumab, catumaxomab). It is a compact reference for how format decisions map onto clinical behavior and the barriers — antigen heterogeneity, off-tumor toxicity, immunosuppression — still limiting solid-tumor engagers.

DICE score: 2 — A well-structured but non-novel landscape review linking bispecific format to clinical properties across approved solid-tumor agents.

MD Anderson Cancer Center, Investigational Cancer Therapeutics.

## Notable Preprints

### 12. Droplet single-cell CRISPR screens identify regulators of T cell-mediated target-cell killing

Saronio G, Antonini G, Jain A, Vogel I, Teneggi R, Neumann J, Zoppi G, Ding Y, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.06.23.734054](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.23.734054)

Interaction-resolved pooled CRISPR screen pairing single CD8 T cells with tumor cells, validated in bispecific-engager settings.

The platform encapsulates individual primary human CD8 T cells with cancer cells in droplets, reads out rapid target-cell death, and recovers sgRNAs from phenotype-sorted droplets — linking per-cell perturbations to killing outcomes that pooled screens cannot resolve. It recovered known and new negative regulators (PTEN, RASA2, FOXO1, AFAP1L2, mTORC1 components) and showed selected hits modulate killing specifically across bispecific-engager and TCR-engineered modalities, with transient mTORC1 inhibition shifting T cells toward rapid cytotoxic execution and better in vivo antitumor activity. This gives an engager-relevant functional-genomics tool and actionable nodes for enhancing engager-redirected killing.

DICE score: 3 — A novel interaction-resolved screening method that maps cytotoxicity regulators directly in bispecific-engager settings and nominates a druggable mTORC1 switch for stronger redirected killing.

Institute for Research in Biomedicine (Geiger lab).

### 13. Visualizing immunoreceptor forces and their effects in vivo

Li M, Lyu J, Li K, Dravid A, Balasubramani D, Kazemipour Ashkezari AH, Choi HK, Kwong GA, Singh A, Zhu C

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.06.26.734558](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.26.734558)

Molecular-tension tools to visualize immunoreceptor (TCR/synapse) forces in vivo.

The authors adapt molecular-force sensors to report the piconewton-scale forces immunoreceptors experience during antigen engagement in a living context, a regime previously accessible only in vitro. Because engager-driven cytotoxicity depends on synapse mechanics and force-tuned TCR triggering, in vivo force readouts could become a mechanistic assay for how engager geometry and affinity translate into productive killing.

DICE score: 2 — An enabling mechanobiology method for immune-synapse forces in vivo, field-adjacent to engager mechanism but not itself engager engineering, at preprint stage.

Georgia Tech (Zhu lab).

### 14. RNA-Encoded PGT121-LS Anti-HIV Antibody: Comprehensive Preclinical Characterization and Translational Pharmacokinetics

Tolksdorf F, Nelke J, Johannson R, Caesar J, Chaturvedi A, Kopp A, Fischer L, Malz A, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.06.24.734219](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.24.734219)

LNP-formulated mRNA encoding a half-life-extended (LS) broadly neutralizing antibody, with translational PK.

The work characterizes in vivo expression, function, and pharmacokinetics of an mRNA-encoded, LS-modified bNAb delivered by lipid nanoparticle, sidestepping the manufacturing burden of recombinant antibody production. ### 15. T cell receptor-ligand affinity quantitatively tunes transcriptome remodelling in vivo, inversely regulating cell division and interferon response

Panova V, Muir A, Armingol E, Burton O, Quantrill J, Lewis DA, Cook SJ, Turner MJ, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.06.24.734351](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.24.734351)

In vivo mapping of how TCR–ligand affinity quantitatively shapes T-cell transcriptional programs.

By varying TCR–ligand affinity in vivo, the study shows engagement strength quantitatively tunes the transcriptome, inversely coupling proliferation and interferon programs. For engager design, this reinforces that the affinity of the T-cell-engaging arm is not a simple "stronger is better" lever but sets distinct functional programs — a consideration for tuning CD3-side affinity to balance expansion against effector output.

DICE score: 2 — A quantitative in vivo dissection of how engagement affinity partitions T-cell programs, conceptually informative for tuning the CD3-binding arm of engagers.

Babraham Institute / Wellcome Sanger Institute.

## Weekly Themes

Engager-specific architecture papers were sparse this week, and the strongest engineering signal came from reimagining how the engaging module is built and delivered rather than from new soluble bispecific proteins. The standout is a protein-free, cell-surface chemical-assembly route that self-constructs a BiTE-mimetic on tumor PD-L1, converting engager construction into a spatially self-restricting bioorthogonal-chemistry problem; alongside it, an all-nanobody dual-module CAR-T that locally secretes an anti-CTLA-4 VHH — reaching a first glioblastoma patient — shows the same instinct to localize activity and shrink binders. Single-domain and compact-format engineering recurred throughout (VHH-only BAFF-R CARs, computationally stabilized nanobody scaffolds, a butelase-ligated Fab–ADC with albumin-binding half-life extension), underscoring the field's continued move away from bulky scFv/IgG formats toward small, defined, PK-tunable building blocks. Clinically, the week was dominated by mature bispecific data: GPRC5D-directed talquetamab outperforming BCMA engagers in plasma cell leukemia, CD20 × CD3 glofitamab nearly halving mortality after CAR-T failure, and a HER2 × HER3 signaling-blocking bispecific opening a new indication — collectively sharpening questions of antigen choice, sequencing, and mechanism. Preprint activity clustered in engager-adjacent mechanism and enabling methods (an interaction-resolved CRISPR screen validated in bispecific settings, in vivo immunoreceptor-force imaging, affinity-tuned T-cell programming, and mRNA-encoded antibody delivery) rather than in core engager architecture.
