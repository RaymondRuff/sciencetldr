## Top Papers

### 1. T cell-mediated antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity provides a therapeutic approach for solid cancers

Richter A, Dotan S, Shendrik P, Rasoulouniriana D, Zarfin A, Santana-Magal N, et al.

*JCI Insight*

[10.1172/jci.insight.205984](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.205984)

Any IgG-opsonised tumour antigen × CD8 T cell; a modified FcγRI receptor expressed in T cells rather than a soluble bispecific.

The chronic problem for engager design is not potency but the absence of binary tumour-restricted antigens, and this paper attacks the problem from the signalling side instead of the binder side. Starting from an observation that CD8 T cells at inflammatory sites trogocytose FcγRI from monocytes and transiently kill by antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, the authors measured the activation threshold and found that FcγRI phosphorylation only occurs above roughly thirty antigen molecules per square micrometre — a genuine density switch rather than a graded response. Engineering that receptor into peripheral blood CD8 T cells produced cells that killed tumour cells while sparing healthy tissue carrying the same antigen at physiological density, and the receptor also reduced basement-membrane adhesion and improved tumour infiltration. For anyone building engagers, the transferable claim is that antigen density, not antigen identity, can be made the selectivity filter, and that the threshold is a property of the signalling module that can be tuned.

DICE score: 4 — Converts antigen density into an engineered, measurable activation threshold for redirected killing, which is a different route to the therapeutic window than affinity or format tuning.

Tel Aviv University with Dustin (Kennedy Institute) and Spitzer (UCSF/Gladstone).

### 2. A domain library approach for generating antibodies that recruit T-cells with enhanced cytotoxicity using new bispecific bivalent antibodies

Sugiyama A, Nakazawa H, Ito T, Murakami A, Kishimoto H, Asano R, et al.

*New Biotechnology*

[10.1016/j.nbt.2026.11.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nbt.2026.11.003)

Tumour antigen × CD3 in the bivalent bispecific 'BiBian' framework; VHH mutant libraries grafted directly into the single-domain position.

Affinity is a poor proxy for redirected killing, yet almost every discovery campaign still selects on affinity first and tests cytotoxicity on a handful of survivors. Here the single-domain arm is treated as a swappable cassette: mutant VHH libraries are grafted straight onto the assembled BiBian scaffold so that every clone is screened in the final bivalent format for function, not for binding. Where the conventional two-step route carried only six of two hundred and sixty-four clones through to a cytotoxicity assay, the functional-first route tested all of them, and three of the resulting molecules reached half-maximal killing at fifty picomolar against three hundred picomolar for the best affinity-selected candidate. It remains a proof of concept on one target pair, and throughput now limits library size rather than the other way round, but it is the right correction to a structural flaw in engager discovery.

DICE score: 4 — Makes format-context cytotoxicity the primary selection criterion for an engager's binding arm, with a six-fold potency gain over affinity-first selection on the same starting library.

Tohoku University, Kumagai and Umetsu groups.

### 3. An mRNA-encoded bispecific T-cell engager targeting muscle-specific tyrosine kinase for myasthenia gravis

Huang Y, Zhang J, Yu H, Zhang J, Zhao Y, Xu D, et al.

*Pharmacological Research*

[10.1016/j.phrs.2026.109733](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.phrs.2026.109733)

MuSK extracellular domain × CD3 scFv; mRNA-LNP encoded, liver-expressed secreted engager targeting anti-MuSK B cell receptors.

Two design choices here are worth copying. First, the tumour-binding arm is replaced by an autoantigen's own extracellular domain, so the molecule selects B cells by their receptor's specificity rather than by a lineage marker — a bait-as-paratope approach that spares the non-target B cell pool, which the in vivo data support. Second, the whole engager is delivered as mRNA in lipid nanoparticles and translated in the liver, trading a manufactured protein for a dosed transcript and sidestepping the usual short-half-life infusion problem of tandem scFv formats. The result that matters for translation is that killing persisted at physiological concentrations of circulating anti-MuSK autoantibody, which is the obvious failure mode for an autoantigen-armed engager, since patient antibody should compete for the same bait. Efficacy is still read out in a BCR-transduced tumour model in PBMC-engrafted mice rather than in autoimmune disease.

DICE score: 4 — Pairs an autoantigen-as-binding-arm architecture with mRNA-LNP delivery and shows the bait survives competing patient autoantibody, which generalises to any autoantibody-mediated disease.

METiS TechBio.

### 4. High-throughput developability assessment of 181 untagged VH single-domain antibodies

Fricke R, Johnston K, Hutton R, Waight A, Ancona M, Sessions A, et al.

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2755649](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2755649)

Camelid-derived VH single domains as the modular building block for multispecific formats; titre, purity, aggregation, thermal stability and hydrophobicity across a sequence-diverse panel.

Single domains are the component multispecific engagers are increasingly assembled from, and the field has been choosing them on binding and crystallising their liabilities later. Merck selected five hundred and twenty-one representatives spanning VH sequence space, produced them untagged — which matters, since purification tags flatter aggregation and hydrophobicity measurements — and characterised one hundred and eighty-one against the internal developability criteria normally applied to full antibodies, plus the five approved VH drugs as anchors. The payoff is a sequence-and-structure-to-developability map, germline and framework features associated with the well-behaved end of the distribution, and machine-learning models trained on untagged measurements rather than literature aggregates. This is reference data rather than a new molecule, but it is the kind of reference data that changes which domains enter a tandem construct.

DICE score: 4 — Supplies the first large untagged developability dataset plus predictive models for the single-domain building block most multispecific engagers now depend on.

Discovery Biologics, Merck & Co.

### 5. Beyond AlphaFold 3: a standardised benchmark of open-source co-folding models for antibody-antigen complexes, from docking accuracy to interaction specificity

Ramon A, Cheruku A, Zhao H, Sormanni P

*mAbs*

[10.1080/19420862.2026.2760737](https://doi.org/10.1080/19420862.2026.2760737)

Antibody Fv and VH complexes with their antigens; co-folding model evaluation rather than a molecule.

Structure prediction has quietly become part of engager workflows — epitope mapping, candidate triage, bin assignment for a second arm — and nobody had compared the current open models under matched conditions. Across two hundred complexes from a newly curated SAbDab2-Bench and a rebuilt antibody subset of FoldBench, OpenDDE gave the best median DockQ and pass rate with Protenix-v2 statistically indistinguishable, both beating AlphaFold 3 while carrying lighter licence terms, and ESMFold2-fast won on throughput for large triage runs. The more useful half of the paper is the specificity probe: decoys built by non-conservative interface mutation and by swapping in non-cognate antigens showed that confidence drops reliably for the wrong antigen but much less reliably for a broken interface, and that OpenDDE in particular stays confident on many decoys. That asymmetry is the practical warning — co-folding confidence is a usable epitope-level filter and a poor point-mutation filter.

DICE score: 4 — Benchmarks accuracy and, unusually, interaction specificity for the co-folding models now used to triage antibody arms, and shows where their confidence should not be trusted.

Sormanni group, Imperial College London and Cambridge.

### 6. CD19-CD28 costimulatory bispecific antibody plus glofitamab in R/R B-NHL: a phase 1 dose-escalation study

Dickinson MJ, Korfi K, et al.

*Blood Advances*

[10.1182/bloodadvances.2026020917](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2026020917)

RO7443904, CD19 × CD28 costimulatory bispecific, dosed alongside the CD20 × CD3 engager glofitamab.

Splitting signal one and signal two across two separate bispecific molecules is the cleanest current test of whether an engager's deficiency is costimulatory, and this is the primary clinical read. Fifty-two heavily pretreated B-NHL patients received obinutuzumab pretreatment, glofitamab step-up dosing to thirty milligrams, and then the CD19 × CD28 antibody from cycle one day ten or cycle two day eight; the maximum tolerated dose was not reached, with pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic readouts collected alongside response. The architectural question for engager designers is whether a CD28 arm belongs on a separate molecule, where its dose and timing are independently controllable, or built into a trispecific where it is not — and a dose escalation that never found its ceiling is an argument for the separable version. Interpretation still rests on a dose-escalation cohort with mixed aggressive and indolent histologies.

DICE score: 4 — First primary clinical data for a dedicated CD19 × CD28 costimulatory bispecific layered onto an approved CD3 engager, which tests the separate-molecule approach to signal two.

Clinical stage, phase 1 (NCT05219513); Peter MacCallum and Roche.

### 7. A Synthetic Benchmark for Topology-Aware Modeling of Multispecific Antibody Function

Southern J, Lu C, Nerli S, Stanton SD, Watkins AM, Seeger F, Dreyer FA

*Journal of Computational Biology*

[10.1177/15578666261499737](https://doi.org/10.1177/15578666261499737)

Multispecific antibody architectures in the abstract; domain connectivity and order as the modelled variable.

Everyone building multispecifics knows that moving a domain from one terminus to another changes function more than most point mutations, and no public dataset lets a model learn that. Synapse is a generative benchmark that composes nonlinear per-domain sequence-to-score landscapes with connectivity-dependent readouts, so the same domain sequences produce different functional outcomes depending on how they are wired together — exactly the signal that sequence-only models discard. The companion graph neural network encodes those topological constraints explicitly and separates formats that connectivity-agnostic multiple-instance baselines treat as identical. It is synthetic data, and the landscapes are invented rather than measured, so the contribution is a benchmark for architecture-aware models rather than evidence about real molecules.

DICE score: 3 — Makes format topology a first-class modelling target with a public benchmark and a matching architecture, filling a real gap, but on synthetic rather than experimental readouts.

Prescient Design, Genentech.

### 8. Early Treatment Failure in Patients With Relapsed/Refractory Multiple Myeloma Receiving Bispecific T-Cell Engagers: Development and Validation of the CHARGE Prognostic Index

Zanwar S, Hansen DK, Pasvolsky O, Tan C, Peres LC, Goel U, et al.

*Journal of Clinical Oncology*

[10.1200/JCO-26-01039](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-26-01039)

BCMA- and GPRC5D-directed T cell engagers (including talquetamab) in relapsed/refractory myeloma; clinical prognostication, no new engineering.

The largest consolidated engager outcome dataset in myeloma to date, and the numbers are a useful corrective: across five hundred and seventy-seven BCMA-directed and three hundred and sixty-six talquetamab treatments at fifteen US consortium centres, median progression-free survival was six point four months overall at eight point four months of follow-up. Clinical, biological and treatment-related variables associated with inferior survival were combined into the CHARGE score and validated in an independent International Myeloma Foundation cohort. For protein engineers the relevance is indirect but real — the index identifies which patient and disease features predict early failure, which is the population any next-generation format, target pair or costimulatory add-on has to beat, and a reminder that approved engagers in this setting buy months rather than years.

DICE score: 3 — Important multicentre benchmark of real-world engager durability in myeloma with a validated prognostic index, but entirely clinical and with no mechanistic or design content.

Clinical; US Multiple Myeloma Immunotherapy Consortium, led from Mayo Clinic.

### 9. Design of a Kv1.3×CD25 bispecific antibody to preferentially inhibit activated T cells and ameliorate T-cell-driven colitis

Deng J, Liu Y, Chen N, Zhang Y, Guo H, Zhu J, Wang F

*European Journal of Pharmacology*

[10.1016/j.ejphar.2026.179529](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejphar.2026.179529)

Kv1.3 ion channel × CD25; IgG-like heterodimer built by grafting the peptide toxin Vm24 into a basiliximab CDR3 with knob-into-hole Fc.

The interesting part is the construction, not the indication. One specificity is supplied by a conventional antibody, basiliximab, and the second by burying a Kv1.3-blocking scorpion peptide inside a heavy-chain CDR3 — a peptide-in-paratope graft that avoids adding a whole binding module and keeps the molecule in a standard knob-into-hole IgG geometry for manufacturability. Both functions survived the graft, the dual-targeted molecule bound CD25-positive Kv1.3-high activated T cells preferentially and suppressed activation more strongly than either monospecific, and it outperformed both in a human T cell-driven DSS colitis model. Inverted, this is a route to adding an ion-channel or other peptide-ligand specificity to an engager scaffold without a third Fv, though a CDR-embedded peptide raises obvious questions about immunogenicity and about CDR3 as a load-bearing site.

DICE score: 3 — A transferable format trick, grafting a functional blocking peptide into a CDR3 to make a bispecific without adding a binding domain, demonstrated through to in vivo efficacy.

Nanjing University.

### 10. Lipid-rich ascites reprograms the T cell lipid metabolic transcriptome to drive dysfunction

Wan PK, Albayrak G, Bruchner PE, Yan S, O'Mahony LF, Fisher KD, Seymour LW

*Cancer Immunology Research*

[10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-26-0206](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-26-0206)

Locoregional BiTE therapy in malignant ascites; effector-side mechanism rather than molecular engineering.

Intraperitoneal engager dosing is attractive because it puts the molecule where the tumour is, but it also puts the redirected T cell into ascitic fluid, and this work characterises what that fluid does to the effector. Transcriptomic profiling of T cells exposed to acellular ascites showed enrichment of cholesterol efflux and incomplete fatty acid oxidation signatures — a metabolic state resembling exhaustion and converging on impaired plasma membrane signalling — with marked lipid accumulation in stimulated cells. The design implication is that potency engineering has a ceiling set by the compartment: an engager optimised in serum-based assays may be reporting on an effector state that does not exist in the patient's peritoneum, and conditioning the fluid or the T cell may matter more than another affinity increment.

DICE score: 3 — Defines the lipid-driven effector defect that constrains locoregional engager therapy and names membrane signalling as the convergence point, which reframes where potency is being lost.

University of Oxford, Seymour group.

### 11. An Fc-inert mouse-human chimeric variant of DuoBody-CD40x4-1BB exhibits antitumor activity and peripheral immune modulation

Pilones K, Sette A, Bachu M, Yu CY, Nürmberger KB, de Andrade Pereira B, et al.

*OncoImmunology*

[10.1080/2162402X.2026.2758451](https://doi.org/10.1080/2162402X.2026.2758451)

CD40 × 4-1BB; variable domains of GEN1042/BNT312 transplanted into an Fc-inert mouse IgG2a backbone (chi-CD40x4-1BB).

A costimulatory-receptor bispecific is one of the few engager-adjacent formats where the mechanism lives in the draining lymph node rather than at the tumour synapse, and that mechanism cannot be read in a human-only molecule. The surrogate here keeps the clinical variable domains, swaps in an Fc-inert mouse IgG2a backbone so effector function does not confound receptor agonism, and is tested in mice knocked in for the human CD40 and 4-1BB extracellular domains. It reproduced the parent molecule's function and pharmacokinetics, drove CD8, CD4 and B cell activation in MC38 tumours, showed transcriptomic evidence of engagement concentrated in secondary lymphoid organs rather than the tumour, and combined additively with PD-1 blockade. The engineering lesson is methodological — how to build a surrogate that isolates dual-receptor agonism from Fc effects — more than architectural.

DICE score: 3 — A carefully built Fc-silenced surrogate that localises dual costimulatory agonism to lymphoid organs, useful as a template for mechanistic de-risking of agonist multispecifics.

Clinical-stage molecule; Genmab and BioNTech.

### 12. Engineering exhaustion-resistance CD19 CAR T cells to address translational barriers for the treatment of cancer and autoimmune disease

Sayed AAA, Chan T, Bolinger CG, Chauhan P, Chakiath M, Poortman C, et al.

*Science Advances*

[10.1126/sciadv.aej2663](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aej2663)

CD19 CAR with membrane-bound IL-15, PD-1-targeting microRNAs and an inducible safety switch, all on one non-viral transposon (PRGN-3008).

Relevant to engager work as a controlled dissection of what a redirected T cell needs beyond antigen recognition. Putting membrane-bound IL-15 and PD-1 knockdown on the same cassette preserved polyfunctionality and sustained killing across repeated tumour challenges in a way that neither modification achieved alone, and transcriptomics tied the effect to oxidative phosphorylation, mitochondrial biogenesis and glutamine utilisation rather than to receptor signalling — a metabolic rather than an avidity explanation for durability. Overnight non-viral manufacturing without ex vivo expansion still cleared tumours and enriched memory phenotypes, and cells made from heavily pretreated lupus patients resisted terminal differentiation. For an engager the delivery and persistence engineering does not transfer, but the finding that cis-delivered cytokine support plus checkpoint removal is what rescues repeated-challenge function is exactly the gap a soluble engager cannot fill on its own.

DICE score: 3 — Shows that combined cytokine support and checkpoint knockdown produce a metabolically distinct, exhaustion-resistant effector state, which defines what a redirecting molecule must supply or borrow.

Clinical-stage programme; Precigen.

## Notable Preprints

### 1. Binding mechanisms of therapeutic antibodies to human claudin-18.2

Wang X, Fu C, Ma H, Yang P, Yu H, Sun Z, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.09.765403](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.09.765403)

CLDN18.2; cryo-EM structures of the human tight-junction protein bound to the Fab fragments of zolbetuximab and osemitamab.

CLDN18.2 is one of the most heavily pursued solid-tumour antigens for engagers and CAR constructs, and until now nobody designing an arm against it had a high-resolution structure or a defined epitope — only the clinical knowledge that selectivity over claudin-18.1 is the whole game, since the two isoforms differ by very little and 18.1 sits in normal lung. These structures supply both, and reduce the discrimination to three residues in the first extracellular loop that differ between the isoforms and carry the selective binding of both antibodies. For engager work that is an unusually actionable result: it names the residues a new paratope must read, explains why some CLDN18.2 campaigns produce cross-reactive binders, and gives a template for biparatopic or avidity-tuned arms on a small, constrained extracellular surface.

DICE score: 4 — Resolves the structure and isoform-selectivity determinants of a major solid-tumour engager target bound by two clinical antibodies, which directly constrains next-generation arm design.

Sichuan University.

### 2. Antibody design across observed and forecasted antigenic evolution

Park S, Widatalla TM, Driscoll C, Mille-Fragoso LS, Specht I, Gao XJ, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.10.765734](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.10.765734)

EGFR resistance variants and SARS-CoV-2 variant panels; Germinal Multi, de novo paratope design optimised jointly against multiple antigenic variants.

Target heterogeneity is the engager problem this addresses indirectly but squarely: a tumour antigen that mutates, or an epitope that varies across patients, breaks a single-variant-optimised arm the same way viral escape breaks a neutralising antibody. Germinal Multi optimises one paratope against several variants at once rather than designing against a reference and hoping for breadth, and the experimental readout is the part that matters — designs bound resistance-associated EGFR variants and neutralised antigenically distinct historical SARS-CoV-2 variants, including variants deliberately held out of the design process, after screening only tens of candidates per campaign. The more provocative demonstration is prospective: the broadly neutralising antibody BD55-1205 was redesigned to cover an escape variant generated by an evolutionary forecasting model while keeping parental activity. Designing against a predicted future epitope is the claim that needs independent replication.

DICE score: 4 — Makes multi-variant breadth an explicit objective of de novo paratope design with held-out validation and a low screening burden, which is the robustness criterion engager arms against mutable antigens lack.

Hie lab, Stanford University.

### 3. Engineering Nanobody Conformational Switches

Pejenaute A, Galvez-Larrosa L, Herrero-Alfonso P, Gomez-Rubio E, Merino S, Millet O, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.06.764871](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.06.764871)

Chorionic gonadotropin, tumour necrosis factor alpha and methotrexate as ligands; VHH framework destabilised so that the domain folds only on target binding.

Conditional activity is the single most requested property in engager design and the hardest to build, because most switches are bolted on as protease-cleavable masks or pro-domains that add sequence and failure modes. This takes the opposite route: destabilising mutations in the conserved VHH framework leave a partially unfolded domain that refolds only when it binds, so recognition and the conformational response are the same event. Because the framework is shared across nanobodies the authors argue it is portable, and they demonstrate switches for three unrelated targets plus an optical sensor whose affinity and dynamic range they tune allosterically. The targets here are small molecules and soluble proteins rather than cell-surface antigens, and a deliberately marginally stable domain is an uncomfortable thing to put in a therapeutic, but folding-on-binding is a genuinely new handle for gating an engager arm.

DICE score: 4 — A portable framework-level route to ligand-induced folding in single domains, which turns binding itself into the switch rather than adding a masking module.

CIC bioGUNE, Ortega and Jiménez-Osés groups.

### 4. Rapid generation of antibody conformational ensembles using machine learning

Rao PV, Sommer D, Davidson D, Alberstein RG, Ludwiczak J, Kleinhenz J, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.06.764889](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.06.764889)

Antibody Fv domains; Cadense, a generative model producing conformational ensembles rather than a single predicted structure.

Static prediction gives one snapshot of a molecule whose CDR loops move, and loop mobility is exactly what determines whether an arm tolerates a format change or an epitope shift. Cadense generates Fv ensembles from sequence that reproduce the conformational and biophysical property distributions obtained from molecular dynamics, compressing days of simulation into seconds, and recovers both apo and holo conformations of the same antibody — the test that distinguishes an ensemble model from a noise generator. Fed into physics-based docking the ensembles captured flexibility that single-structure inputs miss. Read alongside this week's co-folding benchmark, the two papers describe the same shift: structure prediction for antibodies is moving from one confident pose to a distribution, which is the only form in which a prediction can inform developability or format tolerance.

DICE score: 4 — Brings dynamics-aware ensemble generation within reach of routine antibody triage at a cost that makes it usable on whole candidate panels.

Prescient Design and Genentech.

### 5. Receptor-guided engineering of oncolytic vaccinia creates a synthetic support niche for off-the-shelf NK cell therapy

Rezaei R, Gan Z, Akeem Akilimali N, Reff J, Lin M, Xiao Y, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.11.06.764990](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.06.764990)

Off-the-shelf expanded, frozen and thawed NK cells plus an oncolytic vaccinia virus armed with CXCL11, IL-15, IL-27, IL-2 and IL-1β, the last two under chemically inducible control.

The design logic is worth borrowing even though the molecule is a virus. Rather than choosing payloads on general immunology, the authors first measured what their manufactured effector product had actually lost — chemokine responsiveness, cytokine independence, durability at low effector-to-target ratios — and then used those deficits as the specification for the payload set, screening under progressively harder effector densities. Vaccinia was selected over HSV-1 by head-to-head survival under human NK antiviral pressure, and the five-factor program sustained migration, survival, killing and metabolic fitness down to one effector per ten targets. Consolidating everything into one chassis worked but produced systemic inflammatory toxicity from constitutive IL-1β, fixed by putting that module under inducible control — a reminder that the payload that rescues function in vitro is often the one that has to be gated in vivo.

DICE score: 3 — An explicitly deficit-driven framework for specifying effector support, with the inducible-control fix for the cytokine that caused toxicity, though the delivery chassis does not transfer to soluble engagers.

University of Pennsylvania, June and Sheppard groups.

## Weekly Themes

The strongest thread this week is a quiet repudiation of affinity as the organising variable of engager discovery. Tohoku's domain-library work grafts VHH mutant libraries straight onto an assembled bivalent scaffold so every clone is screened for cytotoxicity in its final format, and recovers potent variants that affinity pre-selection would have discarded; the JCI Insight FcγRI work goes further and makes antigen density, not antigen identity or affinity, the engineered selectivity filter, with a measured threshold near thirty molecules per square micrometre. Both say the same thing from opposite ends: the quantity an engager designer should be optimising is the functional output in the final architecture, and binding constants are a lossy proxy for it. The computational papers converge on the same problem from the modelling side, and all three move away from single confident predictions — co-folding models benchmarked for interaction specificity rather than docking accuracy alone and shown to be unreliable at rejecting broken interfaces, a synthetic benchmark that makes domain connectivity a first-class modelling target, and a generative model that returns Fv ensembles instead of one pose. Format and dynamics, not sequence alone, are becoming the thing being predicted.

Second, the single domain is consolidating as the field's standard component, and the supporting infrastructure arrived this week: Merck's untagged developability characterisation of one hundred and eighty-one VHs with germline-level guidance on what behaves well, and a route to making a VHH's own folding the switch that gates its activity. Third, delivery keeps absorbing the engager — an mRNA-LNP-encoded MuSK × CD3 molecule translated in the liver, using an autoantigen's extracellular domain as its targeting arm and surviving competition from patient autoantibody, which continues the trend of encoding rather than manufacturing the protein. Fourth, costimulation is being unbundled: the first primary clinical data for a dedicated CD19 × CD28 bispecific dosed alongside glofitamab, with the maximum tolerated dose not reached, argues for keeping signal two on a separately dosable molecule rather than folding it into a trispecific, while the CAR-T and oncolytic-niche work independently concludes that cytokine support plus checkpoint removal, not receptor tuning, is what sustains repeated-challenge killing. Running underneath all of it is a sobering pair of numbers for anyone designing the next format — median progression-free survival of six point four months across nearly a thousand engager treatments in myeloma, and a lipid-rich ascitic compartment that metabolically disables the very T cell a locoregional engager has just recruited.
