## Top Papers

### 1. Shared α-helix chimeraffibodies enable linker-free bispecific protein design

Qiu X, Chen X, Zeng Z, Li Y, Yuan J, Xu C, et al.

*Cell Chemical Biology*

[10.1016/j.chembiol.2026.09.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chembiol.2026.09.022)

HER2 (affibody) × human serum albumin (albumin-binding domain), fused linker-free through a shared α-helix; construct 861CM.

Nearly every compact bispecific pays a tax for its flexible linker: conformational heterogeneity, aggregation-prone ensembles, and a peptide stretch that is itself an immunogenic liability. This work removes the linker entirely by making two small scaffolds share one α-helix, so interdomain geometry is encoded in the fold rather than left to a tether, and the orientation is chosen computationally by AlphaSync — closed-form exhaustive rigid-body superposition to fix domain placement, then ProteinMPNN to repair the sequence across the seam. The resulting 861CM keeps both parental paratopes, binds HSA at ~14.3 pM and HER2 at 610 pM in the albumin-bound state, and in vivo shows sustained tumor localization with markedly lower hepatic and renal background. Caveats: this is an affibody/ABD pair, not an scFv or VHH engager arm, and the method's generality across domain classes and across a CD3-binding arm is asserted rather than demonstrated.

DICE score: 5 — Turns interdomain geometry into a designable, linker-free structural feature and ships a working computational pipeline for it, addressing homogeneity, manufacturability and half-life in one architecture.

### 2. An in vivo T cell engager platform induces deep plasma cell depletion in autoimmune dry eye

Ling J, Wang D, Liang J, Li J, Hu Q, Zhang Q, et al.

*Cell Reports Medicine*

[10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.103125](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.103125)

BCMA × CD3 bispecific T cell engager, secreted in vivo by endogenous T cells programmed with a T cell-targeted integration-deficient lentiviral vector (T-IDLE).

The engineering move here is relocating the engager's manufacture into the patient: a T cell-targeted, integration-deficient lentiviral vector makes endogenous T cells transient factories for a BCMA × CD3 TCE, which sidesteps both ex vivo cell manufacturing and the systemic-exposure problem of infused engagers. A single intravenous dose produced deep but deliberately time-limited plasmablast and plasma cell depletion, reversed lacrimal gland inflammation and restored tear production in a Sjögren's-associated dry eye model, with no detectable off-target transduction; cynomolgus studies showed plasma cell clearance with a favorable safety profile. Mechanistically the secreted TCE recruited nearby non-activated T cells, which is the property that makes local secretion viable rather than merely convenient. Caveats: integration-deficient vectors trade durability for safety, so the depletion window is set by vector persistence rather than by design, and dose control of a secreted biologic is intrinsically harder to titrate than an infusion.

DICE score: 5 — Converts the engager from an infused protein into an in vivo-expressed payload with a self-limiting exposure window, and validates it in non-human primates for an autoimmune rather than oncology indication.

### 3. Multiproperty optimization of therapeutic antibodies through sequence editing via large language model and reinforcement learning

Qiu M, Wang Z, Liu X, Wang H, Hua Q, Shu Q, et al.

*Nature Communications*

[10.1038/s41467-026-78533-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78533-6)

Method paper; exemplified on MET (emibetuzumab), IL-6 (siltuximab) and HER2 (trastuzumab) IgGs.

Engager arms rarely need one property improved — they need affinity, specificity and thermal stability moved together, which is exactly where single-objective optimizers fail. AbOptimizer frames this as reinforcement learning over parallel multisite sequence edits, choosing both where to edit and which action to take (reservation, substitution, and optionally insertion or deletion), drawing candidate mutations from protein language models while surrogate evaluation and Pareto rewards shape the policy. The indel vocabulary is the differentiating detail: most antibody optimizers are substitution-only, and length changes in CDR loops are precisely what affinity campaigns often need. In vitro validation is real but modest — affinity-specificity co-optimization for emibetuzumab, improved affinity and specificity with largely maintained thermal stability for siltuximab, and indel-inclusive affinity optimization for trastuzumab. Caveats: three conventional IgGs is a narrow test bed, no engager or multispecific construct is optimized here, and Pareto rewards depend on surrogate models whose errors propagate silently.

DICE score: 4 — Multiproperty co-optimization with an insertion/deletion action space and wet-lab confirmation on three clinical antibodies, directly transferable to engager binding-arm campaigns.

### 4. T cell-mediated depletion of intratumoral regulatory T cells using a CCR8xCD3 T cell-engaging bispecific antibody (TCB) promotes antitumor immunity in preclinical mouse models

Wang B, Ishizuka I, Esen E, Li J, Chiu C, Sun Y, et al.

*Science Translational Medicine*

[10.1126/scitranslmed.aea7127](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aea7127)

CCR8 × CD3 T cell-engaging bispecific antibody.

This inverts the usual target logic for a CD3 engager: instead of redirecting T cells onto tumor cells, it redirects them onto an immunosuppressive host compartment. The rationale is mechanical rather than biological — monoclonal antibody-mediated Treg depletion leans on NK cells, which are scarce in many solid tumors, whereas a TCB recruits the far more abundant T cell compartment by overwriting its antigen specificity. The CCR8 × CD3 TCB selectively depleted intratumoral Tregs from human solid tumor samples in vitro and inhibited growth across several syngeneic models with varying T cell infiltration, alone and with anti-PD-L1. The tolerability result is the one worth noting: in cynomolgus monkeys it depleted the CCR8-positive Treg subset durably without acute phase inflammation or CRS, which is not what one expects from a CD3 engager against a chemokine receptor. Caveats: all efficacy is preclinical and CCR8's enrichment on tumor-infiltrating Tregs is relative, not absolute, so the therapeutic window depends on how faithfully that enrichment holds in human tumors.

DICE score: 4 — Establishes depletion of a suppressive stromal compartment, rather than tumor cells, as a credible engager application, with non-human primate tolerability that undercuts the expected CRS objection.

### 5. T cell cross-priming by PD-L1-positive type 1 conventional dendritic cells mediates the antitumor efficacy of a PD-L1×4-1BB bispecific antibody

Luri-Rey C, Rodríguez I, Arrieta-Aranzueque S, Guillamón DR, Vercher E, Soria N, et al.

*Immunity*

[10.1016/j.immuni.2026.10.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.10.003)

PD-L1 × 4-1BB conditional-costimulation bispecific antibody.

Conditional costimulation bispecifics are designed on the premise that tumor PD-L1 localizes 4-1BB agonism to the tumor, limiting systemic toxicity. This study largely dismantles that premise: efficacy was substantially independent of PD-L1 on malignant cells, but collapsed in Batf3-deficient mice or on cDC1 depletion, and cDC1-specific deletion of PD-L1 alone markedly limited tumor control. The operative cell-cell interface is therefore cDC1–T cell, not tumor–T cell, with efficacy requiring cross-presentation, IL-12, 4-1BB costimulation and CD40L-mediated cDC1 licensing; treatment increased cDC1–T cell contacts and the spatial proximity of cDC1s to activated CD8 T cells, and expanding dendritic cells with Flt3L improved control further. For anyone choosing an anchor antigen for a conditional agonist, this reframes the design question from "where is the tumor antigen" to "which cell must be bridged." Caveats: mouse models throughout, and the clinical molecule's behavior in human tumors with different cDC1 content remains an inference.

DICE score: 4 — Reassigns the mechanism of a clinical-stage costimulatory bispecific from tumor-anchored to dendritic-cell-anchored, which changes how anchor arms for conditional agonists should be selected.

Genmab-associated; Melero/Teijeira groups (CIMA, Pamplona).

### 6. Membrane-Anchored Dual-Targeting Aptamer Engagers for Programmable T Cell Redirection in Solid and Hematological Cancers

Zhang C, Zhou SY, Wang SQ, Yi H, Zhu Y, Wang XQ, et al.

*ACS Nano*

[10.1021/acsnano.6c12746](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnano.6c12746)

Fully synthetic DNA aptamer engager (AptE 2) with multivalent tumor recognition plus a T cell-binding arm, membrane-anchored rather than soluble.

Two departures from protein engager practice at once: the binding arms are DNA aptamers rather than antibody fragments, and the construct is anchored into the T cell membrane instead of circulating. Built entirely from aptamers via bioorthogonal conjugation and enzymatic ligation, AptE 2 packs multivalent tumor recognition, T cell binding, nuclease resistance and prolonged membrane retention into one modular scaffold, pre-arming T cells without genetic modification. Retention is the headline measurement — sustained surface residence for up to 6 h on CCRF-CEM cells with markedly better wash-off resistance than monovalent or bivalent controls — and pre-armed TALL-104 cells drove over 90% lysis with amplified IFN-γ, alongside tumor regression in colorectal models and suppression of disseminated leukemia. Caveats: aptamer affinity and in vivo nuclease stability remain the perennial weak points, TALL-104 is a cell line rather than primary T cells, and "programmable" here means modular assembly, not any tumor-conditional logic.

DICE score: 4 — A non-protein, non-viral pre-arming chemistry that gets engager function without genetic engineering, with membrane-residence data good enough to make the anchoring strategy credible.

### 7. Affinity Engineering Modulates Soluble Antigen Degrader Activity

Algov I, Shue L, Zhou X

*ChemBioChem*

[10.1002/cbic.70549](https://doi.org/10.1002/cbic.70549)

Bispecific targeting chimeras (TACs): soluble target (TNF-α) × internalizing effector receptor (TfR1 or ACKR5).

The useful content here is a set of affinity rules that generalize well beyond degraders. Building bispecifics that shuttle soluble proteins to constitutively internalizing receptors, the authors find uptake depends on both arms and — counter to the reflex of maximizing affinity everywhere — that faster target association aids extracellular capture while *increased intracellular dissociation* can improve throughput provided overall binding strength is preserved. pH-sensitive target binders further enhance internalization, but only when neutral-pH affinity stays high, a conditional that is easy to get wrong. Applying these rules to TNF-α yielded a TAC that inhibits signaling more effectively than adalimumab, and ACKR5 is introduced as a second viable internalizing effector. Caveats: a soluble-antigen degrader is not a cytotoxic engager, so the kinetic optima will not transfer unchanged to a CD3 arm, and the TNF-α-versus-adalimumab comparison rests on a signaling assay rather than any in vivo model.

DICE score: 4 — Separates on-rate, off-rate and pH-dependence into distinct, measured design levers for bispecific arm engineering, which is exactly the decomposition affinity-maturation campaigns usually lack.

### 8. A modular nucleic acid-albumin biomolecular platform for multifunctional drug designs

Fuchs E, Dinesen A, Ong SC, Zhang Y, Anthi AK, Rudnik-Jansen I, et al.

*Journal of Controlled Release*

[10.1016/j.jconrel.2026.115514](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jconrel.2026.115514)

EGFR × CD3 scFv-albumin TCE fusion, with an anti-PD-L1 nanobody module annealed on through complementary oligonucleotides.

Combining an engager with a checkpoint inhibitor normally means either co-administration or committing to a fixed trispecific, and the latter forces a new expression and purification campaign per combination. This platform keeps the combination modular: an anti-PD-L1 nanobody-oligonucleotide module is annealed by base pairing onto an EGFR × CD3 scFv-albumin TCE via site-specific Cys34 conjugation, so new functions attach without re-engineering the protein. The assembly bound its targets, blocked the PD-1/PD-L1 axis and enhanced T cell-mediated killing beyond single or separately combined treatments, and because the chassis is albumin, half-life becomes a tunable parameter — engineering higher FcRn affinity extended circulation in humanized FcRn/albumin mice from 18.8 h to 26.6 h. Caveats: a 26.6 h half-life is still short of antibody-like exposure, duplex-linked modules raise obvious in vivo stability and dissociation questions that are not resolved here, and the data are in vitro plus PK, with no efficacy model.

DICE score: 4 — Makes multispecific composition a conjugation step rather than a protein-engineering project, with programmable half-life built into the scaffold choice.

### 9. Izalontamab brengitecan versus platinum-based chemotherapy in EGFR-mutated advanced non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer after progression on third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors (PANKU-Lung01): a multicentre, randomised, open-label, phase 3 trial in China

Hong S, Zhou T, Hou X, Meng X, Hu Z, Zheng W, et al.

*The Lancet*

[10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02026-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(26)02026-X)

EGFR × HER3 bispecific antibody-drug conjugate (izalontamab brengitecan) with a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload.

This is the first phase 3 evidence for any bispecific ADC, which makes it a format milestone rather than only a trial readout. In 432 patients with EGFR-mutated non-squamous NSCLC progressing after third-generation TKIs, iza-bren extended BICR-assessed median progression-free survival to 11.1 months versus 5.7 months for platinum doublet chemotherapy (HR 0.40, 95% CI 0.31–0.51; one-sided p<0.0001). The relevance to engager design is in what dual targeting bought and what it cost: grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events reached 79% versus 62%, predominantly haematological, though discontinuation (2% in each arm) and treatment-related deaths (one per arm) were not elevated. Caveats: a China-only population and an open-label design limit generalizability, overall survival is interim, and an ADC's two arms serve antigen capture and internalization rather than cell bridging — so the toxicity arithmetic does not transfer directly to a CD3 engager.

DICE score: 4 — First phase 3 win for a bispecific ADC, establishing that a dual-antigen targeting arm can carry a cytotoxic payload to a registrational endpoint.

Clinical-stage (phase 3, NCT06382116); Baili-Bio (Chengdu) Pharmaceutical.

### 10. BTK Inhibitor Ibrutinib Reduces Bispecific Antibody-Related CRS Without Undermining the Anti-Tumor Efficacy

Cao J, Hu D, Lv J, Chai J, Wang Y, Chen J, et al.

*Cancer Science*

[10.1111/cas.70575](https://doi.org/10.1111/cas.70575)

CD3-redirecting bispecific antibodies in B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma; mechanistic study of the cellular source of CRS.

CRS is usually attacked through molecular design — reduced-affinity CD3 arms, step-up dosing, masking — so it matters to know which cell actually produces the cytokines. Patient serum profiling at CRS onset showed IL-6, IL-8 and IL-10 elevated, and both public data and in vitro reconstitution pointed to monocytes and macrophages rather than the redirected T cells as the dominant source; adding macrophages substantially amplified bispecific-induced cytokine production, and in vivo macrophage depletion attenuated it. BTK inhibition then shifted macrophage phenotype and cut IL-6, IL-8 and IL-10 output without impairing antitumor efficacy in vitro and in humanized models. If the toxicity-driving cell is a bystander rather than the effector, part of the CRS problem is pharmacological and need not be paid for in engager potency. Caveats: the specific bispecific molecules are unnamed in the abstract, the patient cohort is observational, and ibrutinib's own immunomodulatory breadth makes the clean separation of CRS from efficacy a claim that needs prospective testing.

DICE score: 3 — Localizes engager CRS to the myeloid compartment and shows a tractable pharmacological intervention that spares potency, informing how much CRS mitigation must be engineered into the molecule.

### 11. Extended follow-up of tarlatamab in patients with small-cell lung cancer after platinum-based chemotherapy in the randomized phase III DeLLphi-304 study

Rudin CM, Ahn MJ, Schuler M, Blackhall FH, Mountzios G, Yoshida T, et al.

*Annals of Oncology*

[10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.095](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2026.10.095)

DLL3 × CD3 half-life-extended BiTE (tarlatamab).

Long-term data on the field's flagship solid-tumor engager, and the durability numbers are what the format was supposed to deliver. At 23.3 months median follow-up, median overall survival was 13.6 months versus 8.4 months for chemotherapy, with 24-month OS of 30.3% versus 14.9% and 24-month PFS of 14.2% versus 2.3%; responses were ongoing in 31.1% of responders versus 3.8%, and disease control lasted ≥52 weeks in 24.4% versus 4.3%. The safety trajectory is the part that bears on design: grade ≥3 events decreased over time with no serious treatment-related events beginning at or after 12 months, and CRS and ICANS occurred early and were predominantly low grade. That front-loaded toxicity profile is consistent with step-up dosing doing its job, and supports half-life extension as compatible with chronic administration. Caveats: an open-label trial with heterogeneous comparator chemotherapy, and these are extended-follow-up data on an already-reported primary endpoint rather than a new finding.

DICE score: 3 — Durable two-year benefit with toxicity confined to early cycles, consolidating the half-life-extended BiTE as standard of care in relapsed SCLC.

Clinical-stage (phase 3, DeLLphi-304); Amgen.

### 12. OpenAntigens: a structure-aware database for antigen construct design across the human cell-surface and secreted proteome

Teixeira AAR, Zhu H, Kothiwal D, Cao R, Mills A

*Nucleic Acids Research*

[10.1093/nar/gkag1051](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1051)

Resource covering 5,328 human secreted, GPI-anchored, single-pass and multipass cell-surface proteins.

Deciding which ectodomain fragment to express is an unglamorous step that silently determines whether a binder campaign produces arms that work on cells, and it is currently done by hand. OpenAntigens consolidates the reconciliation work — UniProt topology, AlphaFold pLDDT and PAE, PDB precedent, InterPro and Pfam domains, mouse and cynomolgus orthologs, paralog and family context, partner and assembly context — into 55,305 construct suggestions plus 148,722 sequence-similarity hits for cross-reactivity assessment. For engager work the paralog and ortholog views matter most: they are where tox-species coverage and on-target cross-reactivity risk get decided, usually far too late. An interactive designer links sequence to structure and confidence metrics with real-time cysteine and modification warnings, and the resource is free with no login. Caveats: suggestions are computational proposals, not validated constructs, inherited AlphaFold confidence is a weak proxy for expressibility, and multipass membrane proteins — the hardest and most interesting engager targets — are precisely where this kind of boundary prediction is least reliable.

DICE score: 3 — Standardizes and de-risks antigen construct design at proteome scale, with the paralog and ortholog context that engager target-arm campaigns need up front.

Institute for Protein Innovation.

## Notable Preprints

### 1. Affinity Maturation Enables Dual Targeting of Cancer-Associated MUC1-Tn/STn and Drives Potent Tumor Control

Ye J, Zhu X, Lu H, Coppola J, Zhang D, Tian Y, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.22.761102](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.22.761102)

MUC1-Tn and MUC1-STn glycoforms × CD3, reformatted as bispecific T cell engagers from affinity-matured 5E5 variants.

Glycoform heterogeneity is an escape route that affinity maturation is not usually asked to close. Using structure-guided directed evolution and yeast surface display on the anti-MUC1-Tn antibody 5E5, the authors engineered single paratopes with high-affinity recognition of both Tn and STn, and co-crystal structures localize the switch to HCDR3 residue 98 — Tyr98 favoring Tn, His98 favoring STn, with both supporting dual recognition. Reformatted as engagers, the matured variants suppressed mixed Tn/STn tumors where the parental antibody failed and were active in patient-derived tumor-like clusters. The framing — functional glycoform coverage as a deliberate design objective, deliberately broadening one paratope rather than combining two — is the transferable idea.

DICE score: 4 — Structurally resolved single-residue control over glycoform breadth, validated in engager format against the heterogeneity that defeats single-glycoform binders.

Scripps Research (Wu lab), with Wilson and Jardine.

### 2. Intrinsic immune receptor triggering mechanism informs rational chimeric receptor design

Wu S, Chen H, Wang Q, Qiang H, Guan X, Zhang Y, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.24.761481](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.24.761481)

Mechanism: CD3ε intracellular domain release and Lck phase separation; format: "TRIG" chimeric receptors lacking canonical signaling modules.

The authors identify CD3ε as the triggering subunit of the TCR, its intracellular domain releasing from the membrane under elevated membrane tension and forming condensates with Lck that recruit CD3ε ICDs from neighboring TCRs — propagating activation to resting receptors, and doing so without requiring its own ITAM. The engineering consequence is unusual: they build TRIG molecules with no canonical intracellular signaling modules at all, which activate T cells and NK cells by ligand-dependent recruitment of endogenous receptors. That is a different lever from anything an engager or CAR currently uses, and the same mechanism appearing in several activating T and NK receptors suggests it generalizes.

DICE score: 4 — A condensate-based triggering model that yields receptors signaling through endogenous machinery rather than appended ITAMs, opening a design space orthogonal to current engager and CAR architectures.

Institute of Biophysics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

### 3. Membrane-mediated cooperativity of antigen receptors and CD2 adhesion receptors in CAR-T cell activation

Weikl TR, van der Merwe PA, Dushek O

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.27.762129](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.27.762129)

Theory and coarse-grained simulation of length matching between CD2/CD58 adhesion complexes and antigen-receptor complexes.

This supplies the quantitative account behind an experimental result the field already has: matching the extracellular dimensions of CD2/CD58 and CAR/antigen complexes sharply improves antigen sensitivity, and nanometre-scale changes shift it by orders of magnitude. The model predicts the activating antigen concentration rises steeply with length mismatch until complexes segregate, after which sensitivity goes largely length-independent, and that below that threshold the dependence is Gaussian with a width of only a few nanometres — which is why such small dimensional changes matter so much. Fitting to CD2-length-dependent activation data recovers characteristic molecular length scales. For engagers, where spacer and hinge geometry set the intermembrane distance directly, this turns molecular dimensions into a tunable parameter with a predictive theory attached.

DICE score: 4 — Converts synapse geometry from a post-hoc explanation into a quantitative design parameter, with an explicit tolerance width for engager spacer and hinge engineering.

### 4. Spatially enforced engagement of PD-1+ T cells with AXL+ cells reprograms the tumor microenvironment and overcomes anti-PD-1 resistance

Yegodayev KM, Perelman G, Shami T, Kaufman B, Raviv D, Marripati D, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.26.761755](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.26.761755)

AXL × PD-1 non-tandem tetravalent IgG bispecific.

The premise is that anti-AXL plus anti-PD-1 given as separate antibodies act independently and never guarantee the two cell types are physically adjacent, so the authors build a non-tandem tetravalent IgG bispecific to force proximity between PD-1-positive T cells and AXL-positive cells. It promoted AXL-dependent tumor–T cell conjugation and outperformed the two-antibody combination both in vitro and in humanized and immunocompetent HNSCC models, with spatial multiplex imaging showing a shift away from a fibroblast-dominated, exhausted-CD8 microenvironment toward dendritic-cell- and CD8-enriched niches. The matched comparison against the free combination is what gives the proximity argument its weight, though bridging via PD-1 rather than CD3 makes the mechanism a checkpoint-plus-adhesion hybrid rather than classical redirection.

DICE score: 3 — A clean demonstration that enforced spatial proximity beats an equivalent free-antibody combination, with spatial imaging tracing the microenvironmental consequence.

Ben-Gurion University of the Negev.

### 5. Refactored intracellular domains enhance CAR expression and function

Bai L, Kumar A, Desai HJ, Li J, de Alwis DP, Hazuda D, et al.

*bioRxiv* (preprint)

[10.64898/2026.10.28.762481](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.28.762481)

CAR intracellular domain redesign; KRGR-ζ1-40, a 45-amino-acid ICD replacing 4-1BB-CD3ζ.

Three rounds of AI-agent-proposed designs refined against accumulating measurements produced an intracellular domain less than a third the length of the clinical 4-1BB-CD3ζ module, which nonetheless increased CAR surface expression and antigen-driven IFN-γ release with no detectable antigen-independent release. Compactness pays off in the delivery-constrained settings: extended expression and killing after transient mRNA delivery, four CARs expressed from a shared mRNA dose, and improved B cell depletion after both lentiviral and LNP delivery. The multi-CAR result is the one that matters for multispecific receptor work, where payload budget is the binding constraint. Worth noting that the agent-guided loop is a design-campaign structure, not a model advance, and that the tonic-signaling claim rests on IFN-γ alone.

DICE score: 3 — A compact, better-expressing signaling module that relieves the payload-size constraint on multi-receptor and mRNA-delivered constructs.

Generate Biomedicines.

## Weekly Themes

Geometry was the week's organizing idea, and unusually it arrived with quantitative backing rather than as post-hoc rationalization. The chimeraffibody work encodes interdomain orientation in a shared α-helix and deletes the linker altogether, treating conformational homogeneity as something to design rather than screen for; the CD2 length-matching theory explains why that kind of precision pays, predicting Gaussian sensitivity to intermembrane length mismatch with a tolerance width of only a few nanometres before complexes segregate. The AXL × PD-1 bispecific makes the cruder version of the same argument — enforced proximity beating an equivalent free-antibody combination — and the synthetic condensate synapses and CD3ε triggering model both locate the decisive events at the membrane interface rather than in the binding event. Spacer and hinge length have long been treated as formatting details; this week they look like primary design variables with a predictive theory attached.

The second thread is a redefinition of what an engager's arms are for. Three papers point them at something other than a tumor cell: CCR8 × CD3 depletes an immunosuppressive host compartment and does so without CRS in primates, the PD-L1 × 4-1BB mechanistic study finds the operative interface is cDC1–T cell rather than tumor–T cell, and the soluble-antigen degrader TACs bridge a cytokine to an internalizing receptor. Taken with the CD20 × mPEG pretargeting construct — which uses an antibody arm against a polymer hapten to anchor PEG-asparaginase on lymphoma cells — the pattern is that "target antigen pair" is becoming a question about which two cells or molecules must be brought together, not which tumor marker to pick. The Immunity result is the sharpest warning in the set: a clinical molecule whose localizing premise turns out not to be what drives its efficacy.

Where the molecule is made and how it is composed also shifted. The T-IDLE platform moves engager manufacture into the patient, trading an infusion for a transiently expressing endogenous T cell and a depletion window set by vector persistence; the nucleic acid–albumin platform makes adding a checkpoint-blocking nanobody an annealing step rather than a new protein-engineering campaign; the aptamer engager drops protein scaffolds entirely to pre-arm T cells without genetic modification; and the refactored CAR ICD shrinks the signaling module enough to fit four receptors in one mRNA dose. All four attack composition and delivery cost rather than binding, which is where multispecific programs actually stall.

On the computational side the week was about inputs rather than architectures. AbOptimizer adds an insertion/deletion action space to multiproperty optimization and confirms variants in vitro; SprintX concedes that experimental antibody–antigen complexes are too scarce to train on and supplies AlphaFold3-derived synthetic priors instead, explicitly as a complement rather than a substitute; OpenAntigens standardizes the construct-boundary decision that silently determines whether a campaign yields usable arms. Taken together they suggest the binding constraint in computational antibody design is no longer model capability but the quality and framing of training data and target definitions. The clinical column, meanwhile, delivered a genuine format milestone in the first phase 3 win for a bispecific ADC, and in tarlatamab's two-year data the reassurance that half-life-extended engagers can be given chronically with toxicity confined to early cycles — alongside a reminder from the CRS mechanism work that the cytokines come mostly from macrophages, so not every safety problem has to be solved inside the molecule.
